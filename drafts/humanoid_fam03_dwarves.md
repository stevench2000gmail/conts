# Humanoid Family 03 — Dwarves (5, exile bands only, no holds/clans as final, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 11. ENY_DWARF_EXILE — Dwarf Exile
Exiles are oath-cut wanderers camped in played-out quarries, forbidden the holds they still dream of. They stand broad and low, bearded, stone-eyed, carrying the shame-tallies they never show. After the empire’s pay stopped, many sold axes to whoever fed. Villagers hire them against brutes no militia can face. They keep exile codes strictly among themselves. In battle they are solid axe-and-shield fighters who hold ground and break only when surrounded. They respect anyone who stands. Work slowly buys parole-tallies.
Combat: 5/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: broad low fighter with hand-axe and scarred shield, quarry dust ingrained in beard, barely chest-high to a human but twice as wide. Heavy boots, tally-stick hidden. States: grounded stance / axe-chop / shield-rattle stagger / kneeling on shield. Variants: 2M young oath-cut / grey breaker; 2F hard exile / older smith.

## 12. ENY_DWARF_FORGE_HAND — Forge Hand
Forge hands tend cold furnaces in exile camps, mending arms for food. Their craft recalls grim forges without claiming runic magic, for their fires are coal and bellows only. Smiths respect their hands and test their welds. They remember hold-forges they will not name. In battle they crush with mauls, slow to turn, terrible at gates. They hold forge-doors to the death. Their aprons shed sparks. Quenched steel defines their pride.
Combat: 5/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: soot-aproned broad figure with forge-maul and burn-scarred forearms, beard braided back from fire, heat-shimmer around. States: forge-brace / maul-crush / burn-cough stagger / seated on anvil-block. Variants: 2M young stoker / broad master; 2F hard smith / older keeper.

## 13. ENY_DWARF_TUNNEL_GUARD — Tunnel Guard
Tunnel guards watch mine-mouths and cellar camps against thieves, masters of dark and timber props. They learned the trade holding real mines before exile. Miners sleep sound behind their lanterns. They hate open sky and fight best under stone. In tight tunnels their short-spear ranks are deadly; in open fields they are clumsy. They never abandon posts. Their lantern-codes mark bounds. Cave-ins haunt their dreams.
Combat: 4/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: lantern-helmed low guard with short spear and prop-timber shield, eyes narrowed to dark, beard tucked. Shoulder-high to a human. States: mouth-stand vigil / tight thrust / lantern-drop stagger / kneeling at props. Variants: 2M young picket / grey warden; 2F stern guard / older keeper.

## 14. ENY_DWARF_ORE_THIEF — Ore Thief
Ore thieves are exiles who pry rich veins from guarded workings, despised by guards and exiles alike. They assay by tooth and smell. Fences ask no questions of their sacks. They know every fault and water-seep. In battle they skirmish with picks and flee real soldiers, fighting only cornered. Their heavy sacks betray them. Assayers cheat them routinely. A rich strike funds pardon dreams that never come.
Combat: 3/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: narrow creeper with pick and bulging ore-sack, dark lamp hooded, fingers stained green-black. Low and quick. States: vein-crouch pry / pick-jab flee / sack-drop stagger / pinned on sack. Variants: 2M young creeper / wiry elder; 2F quick lass / older sorter.

## 15. ENY_DWARF_STONE_SINGER — Stone Singer
Singers too old to swing keep hammer-timing songs that synchronize whole crews. Their tunes map safe rhythms, never naming holds. Bands work faster under song. They settle pay-shares with verses. In battle they stand shaking drum-staffs, their weak taps less than voices that prevent routs. Their fall stills every hammer. They teach young the old cadences. Silence follows them.
Combat: 2/1/5/5, always_physical.
Appearance: grey singer with drum-staff and song-tallies knotted in beard, dust-white, seated on stone at a human’s knee. Hands trembling, voice strong. States: staff-raised beat / weak tap / bowed stagger / seated singing. Variants: 2M old singer / blind drummer; 2F old wife / tale-keeper.
