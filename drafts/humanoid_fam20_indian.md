# Humanoid Family 20 — Indian trio Rakshasa/Yaksha/Garuda (5, high-count, outside bands; Garuda name held here, Beast uses Roc), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free, every sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 81. ENY_RAKSHASA_CLAW — Rakshasa Claw
Tiger-striped rakshasa bands prowl dense forest borders lying beyond the last rice terraces. Their clawed fighters revere personal strength and small cairns raised over notable kills. They learned patient ambush techniques from watching great cats hunt deer. Villagers bar their doors quickly at the first roars. They collect heavy iron bangles from fallen foes. They fear swift river crossings more than spears. In battle they rake with iron-shod claws, leaping suddenly from concealing cover. They challenge enemy champions to single combat proudly.
Combat: 6/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: tiger-striped horned fighter with iron claws and bangle-armor, fanged grin, bulk overtowering humans. Loping gait. States: border-prowl crouch / leaping rake / river-balked stagger / cairn-retreat surrender. Variants: 2M young claw / scarred reaver; 2F hard claw-sister / older keeper.

## 82. ENY_RAKSHASA_DANCER — Rakshasa Dancer
Masked dancer rakshasas perform terrifying swirling war-dances immediately before every nighttime raid. Their watching bands fight noticeably emboldened by pounding shield-skin rhythms. They remember ancient massacre sites and revisit them yearly. They demand strong wine before performing. In battle they whirl gracefully with twin short swords, dazzling foes before cutting deeply. They falter badly without supporting drums. Their leering masks terrify village children for miles. Captured dancers laugh defiantly until ransomed.
Combat: 5/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: masked whirling fighter with twin swords and drum-shield, striped limbs blurring, bells jingling. States: war-dance spin / dazzling twin-cut / drum-loss falter stagger / masked kneeling surrender. Variants: 2M young dancer / scarred performer; 2F hard dancer / older drummer.

## 83. ENY_YAKSHA_GUARD — Yaksha Guard
Barrel-bodied yaksha bands guard mossy grove shrines they diligently tend without claiming priesthood. Their stout fighters wield heavy knotted clubs against encroaching loggers. They adore gambling with carved bone dice. Passing travelers pay small tolls in wine. They dimly remember buried hoards from fallen regimes. In battle they stand immovable like gateposts with great clubs, holding sacred ground. They pursue detected thieves relentlessly for miles. Their dice rolls decide nightly watches fairly.
Combat: 6/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: barrel-bodied warden with great club and dice-pouch, belly vast, stance rooted like a stump. Bearded. States: shrine-guard stance / club-rooted smash / grove-fire panic stagger / seated dice-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young guard / mossy elder; 2F hard guard / older keeper.

## 84. ENY_YAKSHA_TRADER — Yaksha Trader
Jovial trader yakshas haul heavy forest goods between isolated bands upon enormously broad backs. Their slow caravans peacefully bridge even actively feuding neighboring troops. They love prolonged bargaining above all pleasures. They readily extend credit to hungry strangers. In battle they defend precious packs with thick cudgels, retreating steadily with goods intact. They prefer ransoming captives rather than killing outright. Their knotted-cord ledgers record every debt faithfully.
Combat: 4/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: broad-backed carrier with cudgel and cord-ledger bundles, smiling tusks, pack towering overhead. States: pack-braced stand / cudgel-swing retreat / pack-cut stagger / seated offering goods surrender. Variants: 2M young carrier / fat broker; 2F hard carrier / older money-keeper.

## 85. ENY_GARUDA_SCOUT — Garuda Scout
Eagle-winged garuda scouts patrol soaring thermal heights high above sprawling lowland forests. Their incredibly keen sight accurately counts marching caravans many miles away. They revere open sky above all earthly things. They despise coiling Naga with ancient instinctive rivalry, clashing violently on sight. They trade molted flight feathers for precious salt. They fear enclosed caves more than arrows. In battle they dive screaming with talon-spears, never landing among leveled spears. They occasionally rescue stranded travelers from cliffs.
Combat: 5/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: eagle-winged tall scout with talon-spear and salt-pouch, beaked face proud, wingspan shadowing humans. Golden eyes. States: thermal-soaring circle / screaming dive spear / grounded wing-drag stagger / eyrie-retreat surrender. Variants: 2M young flyer / scarred watcher; 2F swift sister / older sky-keeper.
