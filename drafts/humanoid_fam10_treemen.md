## 46. ENY_TREEMAN_OAK — Oak Treeman
Rooted giants wake slowly in ancient groves where oaks predate every boundary stone. Their bark hides eyes deep as knotholes, watching woodsmen across slow decades. They remember fires set by imperial charcoal crews and successor clear-cutters alike. Villagers leave damaged tools at grove edges as apology offerings. They move only when groves face axe or flame. In battle they crush with limb-arms, impervious to arrows, vulnerable to fire and axes. They pursue no farther than root-shade. Their fallen leaves bury the slain quietly.
Combat: 7/1/1/3, always_physical.
Appearance: towering oak-bodied giant with limb-arms thick as beams, knothole eyes glowing faintly, roots dragging like feet. Dwarfs humans threefold. States: rooted vigil stillness / limb-crush sweep / axe-bite shudder stagger / kneeling with split trunk. Variants: 2M old oak / scarred oak (male-presenting bark patterns, reason: slow seed-lines read male); fallback no females: bark has no sex, two age-forms.

## 47. ENY_TREEMAN_WILLOW — Willow Treeman
Slender willow giants sway over marsh pools where drowned roads vanish. Their hanging hair hides nesting birds and old nooses. They remember floods that drowned imperial causeways. Fisherfolk leave eel-traps untouched beneath them. They wake when waters are dammed or poisoned. In battle they lash with whistling branches, entangling boats and riders. They cannot leave wet ground. Their strands strangle slowly.
Combat: 6/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: tall swaying willow-form with curtain branches and pool-dark eyes, limbs supple as ropes, towering slender over humans. Algae draped. States: pool-side sway / branch-lash entangle / drained-wilt stagger / collapsed strands kneeling. Variants: 2M young withy / grey hanging elder (same fallback: sexless bark, age-forms).
