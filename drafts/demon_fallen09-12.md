## 9. ENY_SHAMASH — Shamash of the Scales
The ninth Fallen once revealed truth and established proportional justice under open sunlight. After judgment lost mercy, nothing may remain hidden and nothing may go unpunished ever. Ore-town magistrates leave one lamp burning nightly in empty courts, begging verdicts without his gaze. Caravan masters weigh goods twice daily under cloudless skies, fearing short weights he exposes. Ordinary oath-takers raise right hands toward sunrise dutifully when swearing binding vows. In battle he opens every sealed order aloud at once, shaming captains before striking with light. No shadow shelters deserters from his audit anywhere, for noon follows fugitives across whole deserts. Widows debate whether his justice or his sunlight failed first in famine years.

## 10. ENY_VARUNA — Varuna of the Noose

Fisherfolk mend nets with ritual knots dutifully each spring, fearing tangles that betray broken vows. Ordinary debtors sleep with ledger stones beneath pillows fearfully when rains swell rivers. In battle he tightens unseen nooses around oathbreakers slowly, drowning captains before striking with waves. No treaty binds his waters favorably anymore, for each clause conceals three deeper snares. Divers debate whether his depths punish perjury or simply punish breathing.

## 11. ENY_NUWA — Nuwa the Mender
The eleventh Fallen once repaired damage without destroying what makes Creation unique and beloved. After restoration lost humility, imperfection proves everything should be remade from shining clay. Potters leave one vessel unglazed yearly on sills hopefully, begging exemption from her improvements. Market-town surgeons drape mirrors over worktables dutifully before operations, hiding scars from her gaze. Ordinary mothers hide birthmarked infants indoors fearfully when rainbow clouds gather. In battle she reshapes limbs like wet clay suddenly mid-formation, crippling lines before remaking with flaws. Bared scars and shattered vanity prevent her shapings effectively in occupied wards always. Floods and kiln failures thin her gardens seasonally, though deep quarries preserve clays through ages.

## 12. ENY_MAAT — Maat of the Feather
The twelfth Fallen once maintained equilibrium between competing forces with feather-light precision. After balance lost tolerance, difference, unpredictability, and exceptionalism become defects to eliminate. Weavers weigh shuttles against feather standards nightly before dawn markets, begging true measures. Granary clerks pour grain in level scoops dutifully each dusk, fearing heaps that offend her. Ordinary twins dread her festivals most, for matching pairs offend her less than oddities. In battle she levels ranks until heroes stand indistinguishable from cowards, then culls by lot. Feathered tokens and tolerated quirks prevent her levellings effectively in occupied towns always. Scribes debate whether her feather or the weighed hearts grew heavier first.
Combat: 6/8/9/5, cycle (perfect-balance flagged proposal; never final).
Appearance: She presents feminine and youthful, reed-slender with balance-true grace. An ostrich-plumed measurer bearing scales that never settle, feather outweighing armored hearts. Level sudden across distinctions, cull lot-drawn without malice, flight feathered and soundless. States: market-weighing feather vigil / levelling lot-culling assault / token-tolerated stagger / tolerant banished defeat. No variants apply: this Demon's unique identity replaces flavour variants.
