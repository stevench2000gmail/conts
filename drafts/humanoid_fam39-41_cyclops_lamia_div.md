# Humanoid Families 39-41 — Cyclopes (2) + Lamiae (2, F-leaning) + Divs (2, fixed), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free, every sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 152. ENY_CYCLOPS_HERDER — Cyclops Herder
Single-eyed herder cyclopes drive shaggy mountain sheep across lonely windswept high pastures. Their lumbering bands whistle tunelessly through hollowed bone flutes constantly. They count scattered flocks accurately by smell alone. Nervous shepherds steal woolly strays only at night. They love sharp cheese above silver. In battle they hurl heavy millstones with shocking accuracy, crushing raised shield lines instantly. They spare travelers who sincerely praise their flocks. Their vast pastures span multiple ridges.
Combat: 8/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Single-eyed towering herder carries millstones and a bone flute, central eye watery, crook taller than humans. Sheepskin cloak. States: pasture-whistling looming patrol / millstone-hurling crushing assault / flock-praised pausing stagger / cheese-accepting grunting surrender. Variants: 2M young eye / grey shepherd; 2F hard ewe-keeper / older dam.

## 153. ENY_CYCLOPS_SMITH — Cyclops Smith
Soot-caked smith cyclopes hammer glowing bronze inside echoing cliffside forges constantly. Their deliberate bands forge enormous gates and hinges patiently. They judge hot metal quality by taste alone. They trade finished gates to villages for prime cattle. In battle they swing white-hot bars with massive tongs, scalding through armored ranks easily. They dread sudden quenching ambushes greatly. Their glittering slag heaps mark territories clearly.
Combat: 7/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Soot-caked one-eyed smith carries white-hot bars with giant tongs, forge-apron trailing, arms mountainous. Single eye narrowed. States: forge-hammering rhythmic stance / bar-swinging scalding assault / quenched hissing blind stagger / cattle-accepting nodding surrender. Variants: 2M young hammer / sooty master; 2F hard smith / older keeper.

## 154. ENY_LAMIA_DUNE — Dune Lamia (female-leaning)
Lion-pawed dune lamiae haunt crumbling desert-edge ruins lying beyond the last wells. Their alluring bands lure dusty caravans closer with soft veiled songs. They mournfully remember drowned nurseries from drowned centuries. They collect tiny children’s shoes obsessively in niches. In battle they rake victims with clawed lion paws before constricting stragglers mercilessly. They spare outrageous flatterers condescendingly. Their sandy ruins shift dangerously with night winds.
Combat: 5/1/5/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Lion-pawed serpent-woman carries veils and a shoe collection, torso alluring, tail long and powerful. Eyes kohl-rimmed. States: ruin-lounging singing lure / rake-constrict killing assault / flattered preening pause stagger / shoe-offering hissing surrender. Variants: 3F young allure / prime huntress / grey mourner + 1M rare consort (female-leaning lines).

## 155. ENY_LAMIA_MARSH — Marsh Lamia (female-leaning)
Eel-tailed marsh lamiae coil patiently in blackwater pools tangled beneath ancient willow roots. Their whispering bands perfectly mimic lost children’s frightened cries nightly. They understand raw grief better than priests. They trade river pearls for iron mirrors. In battle they drown wading swimmers swiftly with tightening coils, avoiding sturdy boats entirely. They release captives who solve their riddles correctly. Their muddy pools conceal generations of white bones.
Combat: 5/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Eel-tailed pale woman carries a pearl-string and willow crown, voice childlike, coils rippling. Gills fluttering. States: root-coiled whispering lure / coil-drowning silent assault / mirror-dazzled loosening stagger / riddle-answered releasing surrender. Variants: 3F young whisper / prime drowner / grey griever + 1M rare consort.

## 156. ENY_DIV_HILL — Hill Div (fixed)
Stone-hided hill divs squat heavily upon windswept ridges overlooking narrow caravan tracks. Their brooding bands demand regular tolls in healthy livestock and workable iron. They remember arrogant old road-kings from vanished centuries clearly. They collect massive bronze cauldrons obsessively from ransacked caravans. In battle they hurl heavy tors with both hands together, shattering wooden carts easily. They spare traveling musicians generously after hearing songs. Their piled ridge cairns mark successful kills plainly. Wary goatherds detour widely around their ridges always.
Combat: 8/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Stone-hided towering brute carries heavy tors and a dented bronze cauldron-helm, hide grey and deeply cracked, breath sour and hot. Low brow shadows small resentful eyes constantly.
States: ridge-squatting toll demand / tor-hurling cart-smashing assault / music-soothed pausing stagger / toll-accepting grunting surrender. Variants: 2M young hurler / grey ridge-lord; 2F hard hillwife / older keeper.

## 157. ENY_DIV_ENCHANTER — Div Enchanter (fixed; tricks only, no true sorcery)
Smoke-voiced enchanter divs bluff nervous travelers with bright colored powders and practiced ventriloquism. Their supposed magic remains simple misdirection and drugged wine, flagged never as final sorcery. Subordinate bands obey their drifting smoke signals promptly. They extort isolated villages mercilessly each season. In battle they blind victims with choking powder before crushing with knotted clubs, collapsing helplessly without surprise advantage. Their mixed powders dampen uselessly in rain quickly. Their practiced ventriloquism fools only frightened children reliably. Exposed tricksters flee howling into hills immediately.
Combat: 5/1/5/3, cycle (powder-then-crush, mundane).
Appearance: Smoke-voiced bulky trickster carries bandoliered powder-horns and a hollowed club, voice layered unnaturally through tubes, fingers stained brightly always. False third eye glares painted on forehead.
States: smoke-puffing boasting stance / powder-blinding crushing assault / damp-powder coughing stagger / trick-exposed howling surrender. Variants: 2M young bluffer / grey deceiver; 2F hard witch / older keeper.
