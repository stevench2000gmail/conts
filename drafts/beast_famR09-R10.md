## 47. ENY_SOBEK_CROC — Sobek River Croc
Massive river crocs dominate delta channels where catfish gather thickest before autumn spawning runs. They float like drifting logs patiently, exploding upward when antelope lower heads to drink. After ferry guilds raised crossing tolls steeply, their unbothered reaches became smuggler highways at night. Smugglers grease hulls with tallow nightly, sliding silently past listening armored giants without lanterns. Ordinary fisherfolk sacrifice first catches willingly, tossing entrails to distract guards from nets. In battle they capsize skiffs violently, seizing rowers with rolling death-spins that drown quickly. Barbed harpoons aimed behind forelegs deter charges effectively, though only veterans throw true. Bounty hunters thin giants near villages yearly, while sacred backwaters shelter breeders untouched always.
Combat: 6/1/1/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Armored river giant stretches boat-long, ridged back breaking water like a reef. Jaws bristle with mismatched teeth, eyes periscoped amber, tail sculls powerfully without visible effort. States: log-drifting silent ambush / capsizing rolling death-spin assault / harpoon-deterred submerging stagger / belly-up beached defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via bulk, reason: sobek sexes differ in size and jaw breadth): reed-green juvenile floater / channel elder giant male / broad-jawed nesting female / tail-notched smuggler's bane.

## 48. ENY_MARSH_CROC — Mottled Marsh Croc
Mottled marsh crocs haunt peat bogs where black water hides every submerged shape from herons. They nudge duck decoys curiously, learning to avoid hunters' blinds after one stinging shot. After peat cutters linked isolated pools with ditches, their hunting ranges expanded across whole mires. Cutters pole narrow punts carefully each dawn, probing ahead for telltale ripples before advancing. Ordinary egg collectors risk fingers yearly for prized clutches that fetch high market prices. In battle they surge from black water suddenly, slamming shins with sideways tail sweeps powerfully. Raised platforms and linked arms prevent drags effectively during crossings through occupied bogs. Otter packs harass juveniles constantly, though deep peat holes preserve breeders through hard winters.
Combat: 5/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Compact mottled hunter blends perfectly with peat stains, shorter than delta cousins considerably. Snout broad and mud-caked, teeth yellowed, back patterned irregularly brown and olive. States: bog-submerged rippleless waiting / surging tail-sweeping slam assault / platform-foiled milling stagger / otter-harried beached defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via snout, reason: marsh sexes differ in snout width): peat-brown juvenile lurker / black-water elder / broad-snout male-presenting / narrow-snout nesting female.

## 49. ENY_DELTA_WYRM — Grey Delta Wyrm
Serpentine delta wyrms coil inside drowned forests where cypress knees pierce slow brown currents. They swallow goats whole seasonally, leaving hoof piles beneath favored basking snags as warnings. After flood years drowned riverside granaries uphill, their well-fed coils blocked ferry channels for weeks. Ferrymen burn tar barrels upstream nightly, herding shy giants away from crossings with smoke. Ordinary delta folk worship shed skins as flood charms while dreading living coils utterly. In battle they constrict skiffs directly, splintering hulls with muscular riverine coils rapidly. Fire-tipped pikes jabbed at sensitive snouts force releases reliably, a tactic every guard learns. Droughts strand giants in shrinking pools yearly, allowing hunters to spear weakened monsters safely.
Combat: 7/1/1/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Enormous grey river serpent bulks barrel-thick and stretches longer than two skiffs. Scales plated like roof tiles, head blunt and scarred, musk hangs heavy around basking snags. States: snag-coiled drowned-forest ambush / constricting hull-splintering coil assault / fire-piked snout-burned stagger / drought-stranded gasping defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via girth, reason: wyrm sexes differ in bulk and head scars): grey juvenile coiler / drowned-forest elder male / deep-bellied nesting female / tar-scarred channel keeper.

## 50. ENY_MUD_WYRM — Brown Mud Wyrm
Stout mud wyrms wallow in tidal estuaries where freshwater meets salt in churning brown lines. They ambush mullet schools patiently, herding flashes of silver into reed corners before striking. After oyster farmers staked entire flats with poles, their scarred coils learned to avoid fenced beds. Farmers wade with sounding poles hourly, thumping warnings that drive wyrms into deeper channels. Ordinary mudlarks gather shed teeth for charms while steering clear of living tails always. In battle they thrash across shallows violently, stunning waders with sweeping muscular tail blows. Netted barriers and high boats prevent approaches effectively during harvests in occupied estuaries. Cold snaps torpor giants reliably each winter, allowing safe dredging of channels for trade.
Combat: 6/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Thick brown estuary muscle feels segmented powerfully, blunt head scarred from oyster shells. Fins vestigial along flanks, tail paddles broadly, breath fishy and audible many paces. States: line-patrolling herding ambush / thrashing tail-sweeping stun assault / net-barred milling stagger / cold-torpid beached defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via length, reason: mud wyrm sexes differ in length): brown juvenile wallower / estuary elder herder male / broad-bellied nesting female / shell-scarred pole-shy keeper.
