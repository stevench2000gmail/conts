## 7. ENY_CENTIPEDE_HUNTER — Red Centipede Hunter
Swift red hunters patrol damp foundation cracks where cockroaches gather thickest after rainy weather. They strike sideways without warning, pinning prey with hooked fangs before venom stills all struggling. After granary cats were conscripted for siege rations, their numbers surged inside market-town storehouses. Storehouse clerks shake flour sacks vigorously each dawn, dislodging hunters into waiting oil pans. Ordinary folk respect their vermin-killing service while keeping beds well clear of floor cracks. In battle they sprint across boots rapidly, lashing exposed ankles with burning venomous bites repeatedly. Thick woolen socks and raised sleeping cots prevent most bites effectively during infested summer months. Scalding water poured along skirting boards destroys nests reliably, though careful sweeping prevents quick return.
Combat: 4/1/3/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Slender rust-red hunter stretches forearm-long, flattened plates gleaming like lacquered armor scales. Long antennae test air constantly, fangs curve inward sharply, legs blur with startling speed. States: crack-patrolling motionless ambush / sprinting ankle-lashing bite assault / scald-driven curling stagger / severed motionless defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, size-forms only, reason: hunter sexes unreadable at combat speed): bright juvenile runner / dark elder hunter / long-antenna male-presenting / broad-plate female-presenting.

## 8. ENY_GIANT_HOUSE_CENTIPEDE — Pale House Centipede
Pale house centipedes haunt high ceiling corners where warm air carries flies upward every evening. They hang inverted for hours, trembling faintly before sprinting across plaster with impossible lightness. After thatch was replaced by tile in river-port inns, their papery egg sacs multiplied behind beams. Innkeepers tap ceilings with long poles nightly, herding shy hunters back toward stable flyways. Ordinary guests shriek at their feathery appearance while landlords praise their tireless mosquito hunting. In battle they drop onto heads suddenly, scattering across shoulders with dozens of tickling legs. Cedar oil wiped along beams repels them gently without killing useful resident hunters outright. Sealed cracks and screened windows reduce numbers naturally, though total removal invites worse biting flies.
Combat: 3/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Delicate grey-pale horror spans a full hand, feathery legs radiating like frost crystals outward. Body narrow as a quill, eyes clustered darkly, movement utterly silent and startlingly fast. States: ceiling-hung inverted vigil / dropping shoulder-scattering tickle assault / cedar-driven fleeing stagger / crushed curled defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, fringe-forms only, reason: house centipede sexes invisible without lenses): glassy juvenile skitterer / grey elder ceiling-keeper / long-fringe male-presenting / stout-bodied female-presenting.

## 9. ENY_CARRION_BEETLE — Black Carrion Beetle
Squat black beetles gather swiftly wherever battlefield dead lie untended beyond village boundaries overnight. They strip soft tissue efficiently within days, leaving clean bone that priests can gather properly. After successor skirmishes littered green valley roads, their humming swarms became a dreaded march omen. Grave-town sextons pay children per jar of adults, reducing swarms before summer fever season arrives. Ordinary folk loathe their stench yet value their cleansing service during livestock plague years. In battle they cling to shins stubbornly, prying at boot seams with horned digging heads. Strong lye soap washed on leather discourages clinging, while torches scatter feeding clusters rapidly. Deep burial with quicklime prevents gatherings entirely, a sanitation law every settlement now enforces strictly.
Combat: 3/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Broad black beetle bulks fist-sized, domed shell gleaming like polished coal in sunlight. Horned head wedges powerfully, legs dig strongly, wing-cases buzz loudly when disturbed suddenly. States: corpse-clustering humming feast / shin-clinging prying assault / torch-scattered buzzing stagger / overturned leg-waving defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, horn-forms only, reason: beetle sexes differ only by horn size): smooth juvenile grub-roller / coal-black elder cleanser / long-horn male-presenting / broad-shell female-presenting.

## 10. ENY_GRAVE_BEETLE — Copper Grave Beetle
Copper grave beetles tunnel beneath old churchyards where coffin wood has softened into dark soil. They carry carrion crumbs downward constantly, feeding blind larvae that glow faintly in deep chambers. After flood exposed pauper graves near wetland towns, their metallic swarms alarmed superstitious ferrymen greatly. Ferrymen hang wormwood bundles at prows, believing bitter scent conceals the smell of passengers. Ordinary mourners mistake their underground humming for restless spirits, leaving extra offerings at stones. In battle they burst from disturbed earth, crawling rapidly toward warmth with nipping mandibles raised. Stamped earth and packed gravel paths prevent eruptions effectively around tended burial plots. Turned chickens root out larvae efficiently, though priests forbid flocks inside consecrated ground itself.
Combat: 2/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Small copper-green beetle shines metallic, ovoid body compact as a thumbnail and tough. Mandibles lift visibly, legs paddle soil rapidly, larvae pale and faintly luminous underground. States: soil-tunnelling muted humming / bursting warmth-seeking nip assault / gravel-blocked milling stagger / shell-cracked still defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, sheen-forms only, reason: grave beetle sexes indistinguishable in darkness): pale larval roller / copper elder tunneller / bright-sheen male-presenting / deep-green female-presenting.

## 11. ENY_RIVER_CRAB — Muddy River Crab
Sideways river crabs crowd delta shallows where reeds trap drifting food after every high tide. They brandish mismatched claws boldly, backing into rents between slippery stones when threatened suddenly. After toll-keepers banned fine nets, their numbers rebounded along river-port pilings and jetties. Jetty boys tie string with bacon scraps, hauling angry captives sideways into wooden buckets. Ordinary fisherfolk boil catches with fennel, turning pests into welcome market-day stew pots. In battle they swarm pilings aggressively, snipping at toes and pole-ropes with surprising strength. Thick reed boots protect waders adequately, while buckets of brine distract hungry swarms quickly. Drained traps and cleared weed reduce numbers locally, though complete removal starves herons and otters.
Combat: 3/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Broad muddy crab spans a dinner plate, mismatched claws one massive and one nimble. Shell mottled green-brown, eyestalks bob independently, legs splay sideways with armored points. States: shallows-crowding claw-waving guard / swarming toe-snipping assault / brine-distracted milling stagger / flipped belly-pale defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via claw size, reason: crab sexes visibly distinct): small green juvenile / scarred jetty elder male / mottled egg-berried female / pale soft-shell moultling.

## 12. ENY_SNAPPING_CRAB — Snapping Shore Crab
Aggressive snapping crabs guard tidal pools where children wade barefoot during warm summer afternoons. They bury flat in sand instantly, leaving only watchful eyestalks above the rippled surface. After storm tides breached oyster beds, their broken-shell middens marked every high-water line clearly. Oyster divers wear stout mittens now, prying shells quickly before guards return to patrol. Ordinary beachcombers learn their clicking warning rapidly after one painful bloodied fingertip teaches caution. In battle they lunge from sand suddenly, locking onto sandals with viselike crushing claws. A sharp rap on shell rear forces release reliably, a trick every tide-pool guide demonstrates. Baited basket traps thin aggressors near bathing coves, keeping summer shallows safe for waders.
Combat: 4/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Compact sand-pale snapper hides easily, oversized crusher claw cocked like a loaded crossbow. Shell smooth and low, legs tucked tightly, snap audible many paces across water. States: sand-buried eyestalk watch / lunging sandal-locking crush assault / rapped shell-loosening stagger / cracked claw-limp defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via claw bulk, reason: snapper sexes differ clearly in claw size): speckled juvenile hider / bleached elder pool-guard male / broad-bellied female / dark jointed moultling.
