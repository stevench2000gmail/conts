# Humanoid Family 02 — Halflings (5, outside-society bands, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 6. ENY_HALFLING_POACHER — Halfling Poacher
Poacher bands live hedge-to-hedge along great farms they will never own, taking rabbits the game laws reserve for tables they never sit at. They stand half grown and move like field-mice, a craft learned from hungry childhoods after the empire’s granaries emptied. Farmers hang wire for them as for foxes, and catch them as rarely. Their short bows drop hares silently, and their snares feed whole bands through winter. When discovered they melt through streams, for water kills scent. In open fight they loose once then flee, useless against shields. Frost hardens their trade and sharpens hunger. A full woodpile and a blind eye buy more peace than patrols.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: hedge-small hunter with short bow and snare-wire coils, leaf-cloak sewn with pockets, bare feet black-soled. Child-sized beside a human. States: hedge-crouch wait / snap-shot release / dragged-up sprawl / bound with own snares. Variants: 2M young runner / grey eye; 2F swift lass / older keeper.

## 7. ENY_HALFLING_TINKER — Halfling Tinker
Tinker families push barrows of mended kettles between hamlets, singing for suppers. Their road-kinship predates the fracture, when imperial fairs paid for tunes. Villagers love the songs and count spoons after, a ritual both sides accept. They mend roofs and pots for nights in barns. When threatened they swing ladles and hot pokers in panic, brave only in crowds of kin. Soldiers scatter them with a shout. Rain swells their barrow-wheels and strands them. A mended roof earns them winter friends.
Combat: 2/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: barrow-pushing small figure with ladle-club and tune-pipe at lip, patched coat dragging near ground, flour on sleeves. Waist-high to a human. States: barrow-lean offer / ladle-swing panic / barrow-drop stumble / seated on wares with hands up. Variants: 2M young piper / older potter; 2F singing lass / older dame.

## 8. ENY_HALFLING_SLINGER — Halfling Slinger
Orchard slingers guard fruit bands’ camps from stone piles built like small forts. Their hail can strip a crow flock mid-flight, a skill taught to children with green apples. Villagers hire them for fair-days. They love wagers and hate discipline. In battle they hail stones then scatter before shields close. Their arms tire fast. Their river-smooth stones are gathered yearly. Wind foils them utterly.

Appearance: straw-hatted small guard with sling and bulging stone-bag, orchard-dust on cheeks, perched on wall-tops at a human’s waist. Quick grin, quick feet. States: wall-aim poise / stone-hail whirl / rushed tumble / empty-bag kneel. Variants: 2M young guard / older captain; 2F keen lass / older matron.

## 9. ENY_HALFLING_DOG_RIDER — Dog Rider
Dog-rider scouts circle halfling camps on huge herding dogs, herding goats as readily as watching roads. The dogs are raised from pups alongside children, understanding whistles better than words. Strangers laugh at the charge until a ring of teeth closes. Rider and dog fight as one, dogs rushing while riders jab with short spears. Fallen dogs break riders faster than wounds. Their kennel-carts move with bands. Meat keeps loyalty; hunger makes them snappish. A fed pack stays silent all night.
Combat: 4/1/3/3 (rider+dog), always_physical.
Appearance: short-spear halfling astride a herding dog tall as a wolf, whistle-cord at neck, herding cloak flapping. Legs gripping shaggy ribs. States: circling trot / dog-rush jab / unseated sprawl / grounded clutching lead. Variants: 2M young rider / scarred master; 2F hard rider / older breeder.

## 10. ENY_HALFLING_ELDER — Halfling Elder
Elders too old to run keep road-tales and divide caravan shares by counting sticks. They remember famine years when bands ate seed-corn and nearly died. Their judgment decides which roads are safe. In battle they stand waving ladle-staffs, their weak swats less important than voices that stop routs. Their fall dissolves bands into quarreling families. They teach young which farms give and which set dogs. Age beds them in wagons. A heard elder holds a line.
Combat: 2/1/5/5, always_physical.
Appearance: grey shawled elder with tally-sticks and ladle-staff, laugh-lines deep, seated on wagon-steps at a human’s knee. Small hands busy. States: stick-raised order / weak swat / bowed stagger / seated telling. Variants: 2M old walker / blind singer; 2F old mother / tale-keeper.
