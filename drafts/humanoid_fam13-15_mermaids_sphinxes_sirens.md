# Humanoid Families 13-15 — Mermaids (5, F-only) + Sphinxes (5, F-only, ex-bird-maidens) + Sirens (2, F-only, ex-half-bird-half-fish), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free. All-female bands with reason. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 55. ENY_MERMAID_REEF — Reef Mermaid
Pale reef mermaids haunt sheltered coastal shallows where braided channels meet the sea. Their bands comb tide-pools for crabs and drowned sailors’ coins. They sing wordless harmonies that carry across evening water. Fisherfolk trade hooks for warnings about coming storms. They fear deep currents and iron nets equally. In battle they drag waders down with nets and coral knives, avoiding dry land entirely. They release catches for shiny ransoms. Their reef gardens mark territories.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: silver-tailed maiden with coral knife and fishing net, torso human-like above shimmering scales, eyes dark and wide. Hair seaweed-long. States: shallows-song lure / net-drag pull / beached flop stagger / submerged pleading surrender. Variants: 4F young singer / scarred diver / grey chorister / old net-keeper.

## 56. ENY_MERMAID_SURF — Surf Mermaid
Strong surf mermaids ride breakers beyond reef lines, bolder than reef kin. Their bands salvage wrecks after storms. They understand tides better than any pilot. Coastal villagers leave wreck shares on beaches. They collect glass and coins. In battle they overturn skiffs with wave-timed rushes, spearing swimmers. They cannot pursue inland. Their salvage caves glitter.
Combat: 5/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: muscular surf maiden with salvage spear and rope harness, tail powerful, shoulders broad from swimming. Foam in hair. States: breaker-riding approach / skiff-overturn spear / stranded gasp stagger / wave-washed retreat surrender. Variants: 4F young rider / scarred salvor / grey pilot / old hoarder.

## 57. ENY_MERMAID_DEEP — Deep Mermaid
Blind deep mermaids rise only on moonless nights from trenches beyond sight. Their bands follow whale falls. They navigate by pressure changes and cold currents. Sailors call them omens. They trade abyssal pearls for surface news. They cannot endure sunlight. In battle they envelop boats in ink-dark water, pulling with many arms. They vanish at dawn.
Combat: 5/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: pale blind maiden with enlarged eyes and ink-glands, many-ringed arms, bioluminescent markings. Movements slow. States: dark-water rise / enveloping pull / sun-struck recoil stagger / sinking surrender. Variants: 4F young riser / scarred diver / grey oracle / old trench-keeper.

## 58. ENY_MERMAID_RIVER — River Mermaid
Freshwater river mermaids nest in delta reeds where currents slow. Their bands guide lost boats for tolls of bread. They know every sandbar shift. Barge crews sing back to them. They fear dams and mill-wheels. In battle they tangle poles and drag swimmers to shallows, never killing outright if ransomed. They remember kind boatmen. Their reed bowers line banks.
Combat: 3/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: green-scaled river maiden with reed-woven sash and pole-hook, fins small, smile quick. Mud-flecked. States: reed-song guide / pole-tangle drag / dammed-strand flop stagger / bower retreat surrender. Variants: 4F young guide / scarred toll-keeper / grey memory / old bower-mother.

## 59. ENY_MERMAID_STORM — Storm Matron (band-level only)

They demand wreck-rights as price. In battle they coordinate multi-wave rushes with conch calls, shielding young behind. Their fall scatters bands to rival matrons. Their conchs are heirlooms.
Combat: 4/1/5/5, always_physical.
Appearance: imposing storm maiden with conch-horn and storm-scarred tail, presence commanding, older and larger. White hair. States: conch-call command / wave-rush coordination / horn-loss stagger / cave-retreat surrender. Variants: 4F young herald / prime matron / grey storm-reader / old conch-keeper.
