# Humanoid Families 16-17 — Oni brutes (2) + Kappa imps (2), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free. Oni stay Humanoid per cosmology exclusion. Snap-turtles (Beast) explicitly non-kappa. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 67. ENY_ONI_BRUTE — Oni Brute
Iron-club oni brutes haunt mountain passes where thunderheads gather each summer afternoon. Their red-skinned frames tower over human travelers, horned foreheads scraping low branches. They learned gate-smashing serving long-vanished warlords. Villagers leave rice wine at boundary shrines to deflect them. They collect ironware. They fear fast rivers. In battle they sweep roads with studded clubs, scattering escorts. They challenge champions to single bouts.
Combat: 7/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: towering red-skinned brute with twin horns and iron-studded club, tiger-hide belt, breath steaming. Twice human bulk. States: pass-blocking stance / club-sweep road-clear / river-fear stagger / seated with offered wine surrender. Variants: 2M young horn / scarred crusher; 2F hard horn-mother / older keeper.

## 68. ENY_ONI_SMASHER — Oni Smasher
Blue-skinned smashers dwell in quarry wastes, breaking stone for sport and road-tolls. Their bands compete in boulder-throwing contests. They understand masonry better than soldiers. Quarry crews bribe them with cheap wine. They hate bells. In battle they hurl rubble then close with fists, slow but unstoppable. They spare children. Their waste-piles grow yearly.
Combat: 7/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: massive blue-skinned breaker with rubble-satchel and bare fists, horns short, quarry dust ingrained. States: waste-stance heft / rubble-hail fist rush / bell-rung stagger / seated among rubble surrender. Variants: 2M young thrower / grey breaker; 2F hard smasher / older matron.

## 69. ENY_KAPPA_RIVER — River Kappa
Bowl-headed river imps lurk in deep pools where currents slow beneath overhanging willows. Their bands dam side-channels with stones to trap fish. They challenge travelers to sumo bouts for safe passage. Fisherfolk bow politely to spill their head-water. They love cucumbers above all tribute. They fear iron nets. In battle they drag waders down with webbed claws, grappling toward deep water. They release victims who bow deeply.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: child-sized turtle-like imp with water-bowl crown and webbed claws, shell-backed, beak-mouthed beside human knees. Wet stink. States: pool-lurk bow / drag-down grapple / spilled-bowl weaken stagger / deeply bowing surrender. Variants: 2M young wrestler / scarred elder; 2F hard wife / older keeper.

## 70. ENY_KAPPA_MARSH — Marsh Kappa
Mud-caked marsh kappas inhabit reed mazes where channels braid and vanish. Their bands steal melons and tools from farm edges. They memorize every hidden plank. Farmers curse their thefts nightly. They return favors strictly. In battle they ambush from mud with short spears, retreating into water. They trade stolen goods for cucumbers. Their mud-nests hide clutches.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: mud-armored small imp with short spear and melon-sack, bowl mossy, eyes wary. Squelching steps. States: mud-hidden ambush / spear-jab retreat / dried-bowl stagger / offering-melon surrender. Variants: 2M young thief / grey plotter; 2F quick thief / older nester.
