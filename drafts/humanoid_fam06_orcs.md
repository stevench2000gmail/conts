# Humanoid Family 06 — Orcs (5, outside-society bands, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 26. ENY_FERAL_ORC — Feral Orc (reuse)
Feral orcs roam broken country beyond the last fields, living by hunting and road-tribute. They stand broad and tusked, strength beyond any drill, shaped by the same hard hills that breed relentless war-bands without importing their gods. Since the empire’s levies dissolved, they have taken passes at will. Travelers fear their downhill charges. They respect strength alone and despise weakness in their own. In battle they rush with cleavers, breaking thin lines through sheer weight. They respect a foe who stands. Their camps are bone-piles.
Combat: reuse ENY_FERAL_ORC (6/1/2/3).
Appearance: broad tusker with cleaver and hide wraps, scarred bulk towering over humans, shoulders like boulders. Heavy tread. States: pawing charge / cleaver-sweep / shrugged stagger / sprawled on bones. Variants: 2M young charger / scarred breaker; 2F hard sow / older dam.

## 27. ENY_ORC_TRACKER — Orc Tracker
Trackers read trails for bands with noses better than hounds. They find the lost and the hidden for meat-shares. Villagers pay to mislead them with false trails. They learn ground the way rangers do. In battle they skirmish with javelins from scrub, avoiding stands. Their noses defeat disguises. Rain washes their craft away. A false trail wastes days.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: lean tracker with javelins and nose-ring, trail-dust caked, nostrils flared. Loping gait. States: sniffing crouch / javelin-cast / blinded stagger / seated with empty hands. Variants: 2M young nose / grey sniffer; 2F keen lass / older finder.

## 28. ENY_ORC_MAULER — Orc Mauler
Maulers are pit-fighters who crush for sport and hire, breaking doors for gangs. Crowds bet on their bouts in hidden hollows. They were trackers or chargers who loved the crush too well. In battle they grapple and smash unarmed, deadly in clinches, slow to turn, helpless at range. They hold pit-centers. Their fists are maps of scars. Peace starves them into road work.
Combat: 6/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: massive brawler with bare scarred fists and iron collar, bulk straining wraps, jaw set. Towering. States: chest-beat / grapple-smash / shrugged stagger / seated on pit edge. Variants: 2M young pit / scarred champ; 2F hard pit / older matron.

## 29. ENY_ORC_SPEAKER — Orc Speaker (no true magic, tricks only)
Blood-painted speakers lead bands with drums and nettle-smoke, claiming strength-spirits. Their power is rhythm and pain-endurance, flagged never as final magic. Bands fight harder under the beat the way mobs surge to drums without any warp behind it. They wear skull masks. Travelers hear the drums for miles. In battle they drum then strike with staves, rallying young around the noise. Their fall quiets bands instantly. Rain stills their drums.
Combat: 3/1/4/4, cycle (drum-then-strike, mundane).
Appearance: paint-daubed drummer with skull mask and stave, smoke-gourds, massive arms. Voice like gravel. States: drum-beat / stave-crack / mask-break stagger / seated with split drum. Variants: 2M young beater / scarred chanter; 2F hard drummer / older mother.

## 30. ENY_ORC_CHIEF — Orc Chief (band-level only)
Chiefs hold big bands by single combat, eating first and sleeping warmest. Young chargers test them nightly. They claim no laws or lands beyond the next hunt, kept below faction scale. In battle they seek enemy champions with great axes, dueling while bands clash. Their death scatters followers to rival chiefs. Their rock-pile seats are thrones in all but name, never called such. A full hunt keeps peace longer than speeches.
Combat: 7/1/2/4, always_physical.
Appearance: great-axe chief with rock-furs and trophy-teeth necklaces, bulk dwarfing kin, eyes steady. Regal menace. States: axe-ground challenge / great-sweep / shoulder-wound stagger / kneeling with grounded axe. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred holder; 2F hard chief / older dam.
