# Humanoid Family 04 — Goblins (5, feral-only, no females, distinct from integrated settlement, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. All-male variants with reason (goblins have no females per WORLD_BIBLE). Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 16. ENY_FERAL_GOBLIN — Feral Goblin (reuse)
Feral bands haunt scrub and ditch country outside every settlement, wholly separate from any integrated townsfolk, implying no faction change. They stand child-high, green-skinned and sharp-eared, a people shaped by scarcity into spite. Since the empire’s granaries failed they have lived on lifted chickens and ditch roots. Travelers dread their night whistles more than wolves. They share loot by bite-order. In battle they rush with crude knives in shrieking packs, scattering the instant a shield-wall holds. They know every gully for miles. Their camps shift nightly.
Combat: reuse ENY_FERAL_GOBLIN (3/1/4/2).
Appearance: child-high raider with crude knife and rag wraps, mud-painted face, ears pricked. Light as a sack. States: ditch-crouch / pack-rush shriek / scattered tumble / piled surrender. Variants: 4M squeaker / cutter / sneak / boss-kin — no females by biology.

## 17. ENY_GOBLIN_WOLF_RIDER — Goblin Wolf Rider
Wolf riders are goblin scouts mounted on lean wild dogs reared on scraps. The pups imprint on riders and answer whistles. Drovers dread their circling yips at dusk. They scout camps and run down stragglers. In battle dogs rush while riders stab with short spears, the pair fighting as one. Fallen mounts rout riders instantly. Their dens stink of meat and fear. Tribute meat buys brief quiet.
Combat: 4/1/3/2 (rider+beast).
Appearance: small goblin astride a wild dog, short spear couched, rope bridle, trophy teeth strung. Rider’s feet dangle in fur. States: circling yip / dog-rush stab / unseated sprawl / grounded clutching lead. Variants: 4M pup rider / scarred leader / grey trainer / fat breeder.

## 18. ENY_GOBLIN_TRAPPER — Goblin Trapper
Trappers set wire and pit traps along footpaths, then sell “safe passage” they themselves threaten. They learn game-runs better than any warden. Farmers hang them on sight. Their pits take deer as readily as men. In battle they lure pursuers onto wires then stone from cover, helpless in open melee. Their own wires betray them as often as foes. Frost ruins their craft. A sprung pit warns whole parishes.
Combat: 3/1/5/1, always_physical.
Appearance: wire-laden small setter with pit-digger and stone-pouch, thorn-cloak, fingers scarred by wire. Crouching low. States: pit-crouch wait / wire-spring stone-hail / netted stagger / pinned by own wire. Variants: 4M young setter / grey layer / scarred bait / old toothless.

## 19. ENY_GOBLIN_SPEAKER — Goblin Speaker (no true magic, tricks only)
Ash-daubed speakers lead bands with chants and stink-pots, claiming spirit-voices. Their power is soot, noise, and nettle-smoke, flagged never as final magic. Bands fight harder under drums. They daub masks that terrify children. Travelers mistake the chant for war-preparation. In battle they hurl stink-pots then prod with staves, rallying young around the stench. Their fall scatters packs. Rain washes their paint and their authority together.
Combat: 2/1/5/4, cycle (stink-then-prod, mundane).
Appearance: ash-daubed small chanter with bark mask and stink-gourds, rattle-staff shaking, eyes bright through paint. States: chant-shake / pot-toss prod / mask-crack stagger / seated with broken rattle. Variants: 4M young painter / scarred chanter / grey hummer / toothless elder.

## 20. ENY_GOBLIN_BIG_BOSS — Goblin Big Boss (band-level only)
Big bosses are the largest goblins, holding bands by bites and meat-shares. They seize the best scraps and the driest sleeping holes. Young challengers test them nightly. Their junk-pile hoards are worthless to men. In battle they bludgeon with nail-clubs, standing while lesser goblins flee, seeking single foes to crush. Their death dissolves bands into squabbling packs. Rivals from other ditches absorb survivors. A fed band follows quietly.
Combat: 5/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: oversized goblin with nail-club and scrap-metal crown, scarred bulk towering over kin yet still short to a human. Belly fat, arms long. States: chest-beat stand / club-sweep / belly-clutch stagger / sprawled on junk. Variants: 4M young challenger / scarred holder / grey tyrant / fat hoarder.
