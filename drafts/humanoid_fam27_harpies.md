# Humanoid Family 27 — Harpy harriers (5, high-count, female-led snatcher-raiders; distinct from Sphinx singers and Siren lurers), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free, every sentence 8+ words (fixed versions). Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 116. ENY_HARPY_SCREECHER — Harpy Screecher
Carrion-voiced screecher flocks circle distant battlefields patiently waiting for the dying to weaken. Their hungry sisters strip abandoned corpses completely within only a few minutes. They learned to trail marching armies across many successive campaigns. They trade battlefield news to jackals for choice offal shares. They dread concentrated volleys from massed archers. In battle they deafen victims with combined shrieks before diving with extended talons. They abandon assaults immediately against prepared fire. Their cliff nests accumulate stinking trophies over many seasons.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Vulture-winged harrier carries talons and a corpse-strip satchel, voice rasping harshly, eyes constantly hungry. Ragged plumage smells of old blood. States: battlefield-circling waiting posture / shrieking talon-diving assault / arrow-riddled tumbling stagger / nest-retreat cowering surrender. Variants: 1M flock-guard husband; 3F young screecher / prime stripper / grey caller (female-led flocks).

## 117. ENY_HARPY_SNATCHER — Harpy Snatcher
Lamb-stealing snatcher flocks haunt windswept high pastures where flocks graze completely unwatched. Their swift sisters lift bleating lambs whole into cloudy skies. Shepherds fire warning shots into the air nightly. They trade stolen wool to lowland weavers for dried fish. They compulsively collect shiny brass buckles. In battle they snatch chosen victims skyward using harness-straps, dropping those who resist violently. They carefully avoid bristling spear-hedges. Their remote eyries accumulate glittering loot piles.
Combat: 5/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Broad-winged snatcher carries harness-straps and a wool-sack, talons massive and scarred, lift tremendously powerful. Shoulder muscles bulge. States: pasture-circling stalking flight / snatch-lift dropping assault / spear-hedged wing-tearing stagger / eyrie-retreat hasty surrender. Variants: 1M guard; 3F young lifter / prime carrier / grey watcher.

## 118. ENY_HARPY_PUNISHER — Harpy Punisher
Oath-keeping punisher flocks torment thieves who desecrate lonely cliff shrines. Their stern sisters enforce ancient boundary customs without mercy. They remember every insult across long decades. They demand heavy weregild payments in livestock. In battle they scourge chosen leaders with knotted cords, ignoring common fighters. They spare those who pay promptly. Their weighted cords cut deeply through leather. Bribed punishers lie unconvincingly and are cast out.
Combat: 4/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Stern cord-bearing harrier carries knotted scourges and shrine-tokens, gaze coldly judging, wings folded precisely. Beak polished. States: shrine-perch judging vigil / scourging leader-striking assault / bribe-exposed shame stagger / shrine-retreat sullen surrender. Variants: 1M guard; 3F young scourge / prime judge / grey rememberer.

## 119. ENY_HARPY_STORMRIDER — Harpy Stormrider
Gale-riding stormrider flocks follow towering thunderheads across exposed high plateaus. Their bold sisters hunt clearly in flickering lightning light. They read sudden pressure drops with perfect accuracy. Worried herders watch their flight for storm warnings. They nest exclusively among sharp stormglass spires. In battle they dive screaming through driving rain with slate shards, scattering shield lines. They cannot sustain flight in completely calm air. Their glittering nests overlook entire valleys.
Combat: 5/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Storm-grey rider carries a slate-shard satchel, wings enormously broad, hail scars across shoulders. Eyes electric. States: thunderhead-circling soaring patrol / rain-diving shard-scattering assault / becalmed flopping helpless stagger / nest-retreat windswept surrender. Variants: 1M guard; 3F young rider / prime diver / grey reader.

## 120. ENY_HARPY_MATRIARCH — Harpy Matriarch (band-level only)
Iron-beaked matriarchs command entire cliff colonies through screaming decrees. Their seasoned counsel alone determines seasonal migrations. Subordinate flocks obey their gestures instantly. They shelter shivering fledglings personally during violent storms. They demand regular tribute in healthy livestock. In battle they coordinate devastating multi-flock dives using signal banner-wings, shielding young behind veterans. Their sudden fall splits colonies toward rival daughters. Their heavy beaks grey visibly first.
Combat: 5/1/4/5, always_physical.
Appearance: Imposing iron-beaked elder carries banner-wings and a tribute-pile, presence overwhelmingly dominating, size greater than daughters. Voice shattering. States: decree-screaming commanding posture / coordinated multi-diving assault / beak-cracked reeling stagger / colony-yielding bitter surrender. Variants: 1M consort-guard; 3F young herald / prime commander / grey matriarch.
