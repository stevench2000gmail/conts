## 37. ENY_FRILLNECK_RUNNER — Dust Frillneck Runner
Nimble dust runners sprint across cracked lakebeds where receding waters expose insect-rich mudflats. They flare pleated neck frills suddenly, bluffing hawks and dogs into hesitating at critical moments. After drought lowered wells across high plateau outposts, their bluff displays entertained bored sentries daily. Sentries toss beetle scraps downhill routinely, training resident runners to patrol walls for scorpions. Ordinary caravan children cheer their bipedal dashes while mimicking frill displays with cupped hands. In battle they dart between legs confusingly, tripping porters with sudden direction changes and dust. High gaiters and steady pacing prevent trips effectively during crossings through occupied lakebeds. Harriers and flash floods thin numbers seasonally, though burrowed eggs survive even severe droughts.
Combat: 3/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Lean bipedal lizard stands knee-high, dusty tan scales blending with cracked lakebed mud. Neck frill pleated brightly ochre and rust, tail counterbalances lengthy, legs built for sprinting. After highland forts banned sling practice near walls, their sunning ledges became safe parade curiosities. Recruits wager bread crusts on dash winners daily, learning patience from reptilian race favorites. Ordinary goat girls share whey with bold beggars while guarding kids from nipping juveniles. In battle they inflate frills massively, hissing loudly while lashing tails at shin height. Stout shields held low deflect charges reliably, allowing steady advances through terraced colonies. ## 41. ENY_CONSTRICTOR — Green Constrictor

Clearing crews prod low branches with poles hourly, shaking disguised hunters down before felling. Ordinary trappers prize shed skins for boots while giving living coils widest possible berth always. In battle they lunge from branches suddenly, wrapping torsos with crushing coordinated coils rapidly. A comrade's dagger slit along tail muscle forces release reliably, a lesson every patrol learns.
