# Humanoid Family 18 — Monkey-folk (5, high-count; no monkey Beasts), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free, every sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 71. ENY_MONKEY_SCOUT — Monkey Scout
Long-tailed scouts swing swiftly through tangled canopy highways arching above dim forest trails. Their noisy bands can strip ripening orchards completely within frantic minutes. They warn distant troops with sharp barking calls echoing across valleys. Orchard keepers hire slingers specifically to deter their relentless raids. They covet shiny bottlecaps and cracked mirrors above even food. In battle they hurl heavy branches downward before mobbing victims with sticks. They flee whistling immediately at the first baying of dogs. Their habitual trails span many miles of connected branches.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: long-tailed agile climber carrying a stick-club and bottlecap necklace, fur brown-grey, sized like a human child. Prehensile tail curling around branches constantly. States: canopy-swing scouting posture / branch-hail mobbing rush / dog-panic scattering stagger / treetop chattering surrender. Variants: 2M young swinger / grey caller; 2F quick mother / older watcher.

## 72. ENY_MONKEY_ARCHER — Monkey Archer
Short-bow monkey troops loose surprisingly disciplined volleys from wildly swaying high branches. Their chattering bands practice daily upon painted gourds hung from neighboring limbs. They guard prized fruit territories fiercely against all neighboring troops. Rival troops wage noisy seasonal wars over contested boundary trees. They fletch their arrows with bright shed feathers gathered each molt. In battle they volley downwards before relocating screaming through canopy, avoiding melee entirely. Their bowstrings rot quickly during prolonged rainy seasons. Their shattered gourd-targets litter every established camp clearing.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: bow-carrying monkey soldier with gourd-quiver and feather armbands, balanced by a curling tail, eyes keen and quick. Compact disciplined posture. States: branch-aim poised crouch / chattering volley release / string-snap fumbling stagger / branch-retreat chattering surrender. Variants: 2M young bow / grey marksman; 2F keen archer / older fletcher.

## 73. ENY_MONKEY_CHIEF — Monkey Chief (band-level only)
Grizzled chiefs hold quarrelsome troops together through grooming favors and thunderous dominance displays. Their echoing screams settle daily disputes over food and sleeping branches. They personally lead every major orchard raid from the front. They remember fat fruit years across many long decades. In battle they direct screeching mass charges while flourishing iron-bound staffs, dueling rival chiefs mid-brawl. Their sudden fall splits troops into warring splinters instantly. Their favorite high perches overlook entire home ranges widely. Advancing age greys their heavy muzzles white.
Combat: 4/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: massive grizzled leader carrying an iron-bound staff with scarred muzzle, bulk dwarfing troop-mates, presence overwhelmingly loud. Grey mane bristling. States: perch-display roaring stance / staff-led charging advance / rival-wound reeling stagger / deposed grooming surrender. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred holder; 2F hard matron / older aunt.

## 74. ENY_MONKEY_THIEF — Monkey Thief
Pouch-cheeked thieves slip silently into human camps to lift warm bread and shining tools. Their furtive bands trade stolen goods onward through trained magpies. They memorize every patrol gap within only a few nights. Camp cooks hang food higher each passing week. They will trade almost anything for fermented wine. In battle they snatch pouches and flee immediately, stabbing desperately only when fully cornered. Their bulging cheek-pouches betray every single theft. Barking dogs deter them far more effectively than spears.
Combat: 2/1/5/1, always_physical.
Appearance: bulging-cheeked sneak carrying a thief-sack and small knife, tail curled tight for silence, eyes darting constantly. Hunched furtive posture. States: camp-slipping sneaking crouch / snatch-and-flee dashing rush / cornered knife-flailing stagger / screeching empty-pouch surrender. Variants: 2M young sneak / grey lifter; 2F quick mother / older fence.

## 75. ENY_MONKEY_SINGER — Monkey Singer
Howling singers greet every dawn with tremendous choruses carrying clearly for many miles. Their resonant bands effectively map vast territories through answering songs. They mourn their dead with night-long wailing ceremonies. Lonely travelers use their punctual choruses as reliable forest clocks. They hate unnatural silence far more than any predator. In battle they deafen foes with coordinated howling before pelting them with hard fruit, rallying troops simultaneously. Their sudden loss silences entire bands mournfully. Their bright throat-sacs swell hugely while singing loudly.
Combat: 2/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: big-throated howler carrying a fruit-satchel with scarred voice-box, throat-sac vivid red, posture upright and proud. Resonant chest. States: dawn-chorus upright stance / deafening howl fruit-pelting assault / throat-seized coughing stagger / silent crouching surrender. Variants: 2M young howler / grey dawn-caller; 2F loud mother / older mourner.
