# Humanoid Family 24 — Boar-headed brutes (5, high-count; WHF-beastman texture only, no lore import; distinct from Beast boars animals), Phase 1, no JSON — EXPANDED full 8x8+
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 101. ENY_BOARHEAD_TUSKER — Boar Tusker
Bristle-maned tusker bands root noisily through forest loam lying beyond the last orchards. Their heavy heads swing low constantly, scenting buried truffles and frightened travelers alike. They learned shield-breaking charges by stampeding through boar-infested thickets yearly. Frightened farmers dread their ravenous autumn hunger above wolves. They collect dented fallen helmets as drinking bowls. They fear crackling fire lines more than spears. In battle they charge head-down with notched cleavers, goring victims with yellowed tusks. They trample fallen foes into bloody mud.
Combat: 6/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: bristle-maned boar-headed fighter with cleaver and helmet-bowl, tusks curved, bulk broad, snout steaming. States: loam-sniffing advance / head-down gore charge / fire-turned squeal stagger / wallow-seated surrender. Variants: 2M young grunter / scarred boar; 2F hard sow / older keeper.

## 102. ENY_BOARHEAD_ARCHER — Boar Archer
Short-limbed archers loose patiently from forest edge blinds with surprising woodland patience. Their covering bands shield lumbering tusker charges from sheltering trees. They fletch their shafts with shed bristles gathered springly. They guard squealing piglets with suicidal ferocity. In battle they volley briskly then squeal-retreat behind black trees, avoiding melee entirely. Their catgut strings snap uselessly in wet weather. Their stinking blinds betray positions downwind. Broken bows shame them before the sounder.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: blind-hidden archer with short bow and bristle-fletched shafts, snout quivering, ears pricked. States: blind-wait aim / volley squeal-retreat / blind-burned stagger / snout-down surrender. Variants: 2M young bow / grey eye; 2F keen sow / older fletcher.

## 103. ENY_BOARHEAD_SPEAKER — Boar Speaker (no true magic)
Mud-painted speakers grunt winding prophecies over freshly found truffle beds. Their coarse authority rests on scent-reading and tremendous squeals, never sorcery. Subordinate bands follow their dripping snouts toward food unfailingly. They anoint chosen champions with sacred loam. In battle they squeal piercing charges then jab with ash staves, rallying young fighters shrilly. Their sudden fall quiets grunting herds instantly. Their cracking mud-masks bake hard in summer sun.
Combat: 3/1/4/4, cycle (squeal-then-jab, mundane).
Appearance: mud-masked grunter with prophecy staff and truffle-pouch, paint cracked, breath hot. States: sniffing prophecy stance / squeal-charge staff jab / mask-washed stagger / mud-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young sniffer / grey grunt; 2F hard sow-speaker / older dam.

## 104. ENY_BOARHEAD_RAIDER — Boar Raider
Armored raiders ride enormous feral hogs, crashing straight through thorn hedges. Their thundering cavalry terrifies walled villages for miles. They shoe their slavering mounts with hammered iron. Intimidated farriers dread their visits yearly. In battle they ride down wavering lines with ash lances, wheeling expertly for second passes. Dismounted they flounder helplessly in mud. Their ravenous hogs devour absolutely anything. Lamed mounts strand riders permanently.
Combat: 5/1/3/3 (rider+mount), always_physical.
Appearance: lance-armed rider atop a feral war-hog, armor patched, tusks filed, both snorting. States: hedge-pawing trot / crashing lance pass / unseated wallow stagger / mount-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young rider / scarred chief; 2F hard rider / older breeder.

## 105. ENY_BOARHEAD_CHIEF — Boar Chief (band-level only)
Enormous chiefs hold grunting sounders together through sheer weight and insatiable appetite. Their nightly feasts decide the next dawn’s raids. Young challengers test their tusks constantly. In battle they duel enemy champions personally with great cleavers while sounders clash around. Their sudden death splits sounders into feuding splinters. Their steaming middens grow larger each year. A consistently full trough keeps fragile peace.
Combat: 7/1/2/4, always_physical.
Appearance: enormous tusked chief with great cleaver and trough-throne, bulk dwarfing kin, scars layered. States: trough-stand challenge / great-cleaver duel / tusk-cracked stagger / trough-yielding surrender. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred holder; 2F hard matron / older sow-queen.
