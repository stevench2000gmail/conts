# Human Families 10-13 — soldiers / knights / paladins / royal guards (20 entries, LOCKED, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. Soldiers lawful faction-neutral, no institutions. Knights no orders named (Squire replaced Lance Leader). Paladins fighters only, holy-magic never final. Royal guards generic-only, no courts. Taxonomy Human. All magic 1.

## 46. ENY_LINE_INFANTRY — Line Infantry
Line infantry march and hold with spear and shield for seasonal employers. They keep step where less trained militias bunch together. Villagers respect their solid line when it blocks narrow passes. In combat, they are steady wall-fighters, pushing forward and slow to rout. They advance together to the beat of military drums. Their strength comes from the shoulders of neighboring soldiers. Broken lines rarely reform once fear separates their members. Officers place them where a dependable wall matters most.
Combat: 4/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: uniform-coated spearman, shield, marching pack. States: line-advance / shield-push thrust / line-break stagger / kneeling with planted spear. Variants: 2M young levy/scarred fileman; 2F hard shieldwoman/older ranker.

## 47. ENY_CROSSBOWMAN — Crossbowman
Crossbowmen loose heavy bolts behind lines, often recruited from hunter stock. They need little training to kill effectively at distance. Defenders on walls fear the force of their volleys. In combat, they shoot then brace behind pavises with short swords. Alone, they are weak once enemies close into melee. They reload beneath cover while comrades hold the line. Wet strings slow their work and make each shot uncertain. Their heavy bolts can pierce mail at useful range.
Combat: 4/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: pavise-bearer, crossbow, bolt-case, padded jack. States: kneeling aim / volley-loose / pavise-drop stagger / seated spanning bow. Variants: 2M young loader/grey shot; 2F keen shot/older supplier.

## 48. ENY_SCOUT_RIDER — Scout Rider
Scout riders range ahead on hardy ponies, carrying sharp eyes and horns. They count enemy camps and burn fodder before larger columns arrive. Marching columns depend heavily upon their timely warnings. In combat, they skirmish with javelins and never stand fast. They flee dangerous encounters in order to deliver reports. Their hardy ponies outlast the heavier mounts of chargers. Hunger and lameness can ground even the most skilled rider. A warning horn lets distant allies prepare for discovered danger.
Combat: 3/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: light horseman, javelins, horn, dust-cloak. States: far-circle trot / javelin-cast / unhorsed stagger / grounded holding reins. Variants: 2M young rider/wiry veteran; 2F swift rider/older guide.

## 49. ENY_SAPPER — Sapper
Sappers dig, bridge, and break obstacles using mattocks and mundane black-powder kegs. Many come from mining families accustomed to dangerous underground labor. Forts dread the tunnels and mines these workers prepare. In combat, they blast gates before fighting with picks. They are clumsy fighters when caught across open ground. They work beneath shields while others protect them from missiles. Their powder kegs are precious cargo guarded during every march. Floodwater can drown their mines before a planned breach succeeds.
Combat: 4/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: dirt-caked digger, pick, powder-keg, timber props. States: digging crouch / keg-blast then rush / deafened stagger / seated on rubble. Variants: 2M young digger/broad master; 2F hard miner/older powder-keeper.

## 50. ENY_STANDARD_BEARER — Standard Bearer
Standard bearers carry colors that hold fighting lines together through steady presence. They rarely strike, yet armies can break when they fall. New recruits watch their cloth for signs of ordered movement. In combat, they defend with short swords while lifting poles high. Nearby allies rally around the raised colors during confusion. Their loss spreads panic through troops who cannot see direction. Guards surround them whenever battle grows especially close and dangerous. Their cloth is carefully mended after every hard campaign.
Combat: 3/1/3/5, always_physical.
Appearance: tall bearer, pole-colors, short sword, braided cords. States: colors-high stand / pole-defense sweep / colors-dip stagger / kneeling clutching cloth. Variants: 2M young bearer/grey guardian; 2F tall bearer/older keeper.

## 51. ENY_HEDGE_KNIGHT — Hedge Knight
Hedge knights wander between hire and tourney with dented harness and one horse. They own no land, possessing only arms and their mount. Villagers hire them against dangerous brutes they cannot face alone. In combat, they are solid mounted fighters with lance and sword. They pride themselves on charges but become brittle in deep mud. They keep old vows of protection when an employer pays. Their horses eat before their riders accept any meal. A lame horse can quickly reduce a hedge knight to poverty.
Combat: 5/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: dented rider, lance and arming sword, patched barding. States: mounted salute / lance-charge / unhorsed stagger / kneeling with broken lance. Variants: 2M young errant/rusted veteran; 2F hard rider/older dame.

## 52. ENY_HOUSEHOLD_KNIGHT — Household Knight
Household knights guard manors and caravans for steady board and reliable pay. They are better fed than hedge kin who wander between employers. They drill courtyard youths in the basics of weapons and discipline. Households trust their gates and family members to these knights. In combat, they hold doors with disciplined sword and shield work. They retreat alongside their charges rather than leave them exposed. Their livery is plain cloth with no named heraldry. Their service remains personal, built on familiar daily responsibility.
Combat: 5/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: clean man-at-arms, sword-shield, household cloak blank. States: door-guard stance / shield-cut / helm-rattle stagger / kneeling shield-wall. Variants: 2M young sworn/older captain; 2F sworn swordswoman/older guardian.

## 53. ENY_SQUIRE — Squire (replaced Lance Leader)
Squires are youthful apprentice fighters tending horses and harness while learning arms. Chosen for promise, they polish equipment, carry supplies, and keep watch. Households feed them because they expect future capable blades. In combat, they are eager but half-trained with sword and buckler. They rush beyond their skill when a knight requires protection. They guard knights' backs fiercely despite their incomplete training. Their mistakes teach them faster than careful instruction ever could. Veterans eventually shape them into fighters or break their confidence.
Combat: 3/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: youthful attendant-fighter, borrowed sword, buckler, horse-brush at belt; visibly young. States: eager guard stance / rushing cut / knocked-down stagger / seated with dented buckler. Variants: 2M stable boy/page youth; 2F hard maid-at-arms/young page girl — all youthful look.

## 54. ENY_FOOT_KNIGHT — Foot Knight
Foot knights fight dismounted in harness with poleaxes at gates and breaches. Their heavy equipment makes long marches and marshes especially difficult. Forts prize them as defenders wherever walls face direct assault. In combat, they are crushing close fighters but slow to turn. They hold breaches against foes who attempt to force entry. Their harness needs squires for fitting, repair, and daily care. Mud can fell them before their weapons ever reach opponents. They prefer firm ground where their armor's weight becomes protection.
Combat: 6/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: full-harness fighter, poleaxe, crested helm plain. States: braced gate-stand / axe-hook crush / toppled stagger / seated with raised visor. Variants: 2M young squire-raised/older iron; 2F hard fighter/older dame in harness.

## 55. ENY_TOURNEY_CHAMPION — Tourney Champion
Tourney champions live for prize bouts in fair towns and fame. Crowds chant for nameless favors when they unhorse celebrated rivals. They hire themselves as duelists during the intervals between fairs. In combat, they use showy precision with lance and sword. They seek dramatic falls rather than killing defeated opponents. They challenge travelers to contests at mounted passes along roads. Their prizes pay for armor, horses, and public display. A lost bout can empty purses and damage their reputation.
Combat: 5/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: bright-harness rider, ribboned lance, polished helm. States: circling salute / tilting charge / unhorsed roll stagger / seated accepting loss. Variants: 2M young gallant/vain veteran; 2F bold rider/older championess.

Their oaths forbid flight when helpless people need protection. Broken oaths exile them from the trust of ordinary folk.

## 57. ENY_CHAPEL_GUARD — Chapel Guard (fighters + warding prayers, holy flagged proposal)
Chapel guards watch small shrines and burial grounds without claiming priesthood. They sweep steps and turn away thieves seeking easy offerings. Mourners trust their quiet presence during difficult ceremonies and visits. In combat, they fight with mace and lantern-shield at gates. They hold thresholds rather than follow enemies onto battlefields. Their duty ends at the walls surrounding their charge. Their keys rattle whenever they make a careful nightly round. Thieves learn that quiet shrines may still have determined defenders.
Combat: 4/3/3/3, cycle (warding prayers flagged holy proposal per magic doctrine; never final).
Appearance: grey-robed guard, mace, lantern-shield, key-ring. States: gate-stand / mace-swing / bell-drop stagger / kneeling at threshold. Variants: 2M young keeper/older warden; 2F stern keeper/older widow.

## 58. ENY_MENDICANT_PROTECTOR — Mendicant Protector (fighters + mendicant blessings, holy flagged proposal)
Mendicant protectors walk with begging healers, carrying staves against wolves and thieves. They own nothing except bowls, patched robes, and necessary walking tools. Villagers feed the pair when their charity can spare food. In combat, they whirl staves to shield unarmed companions without killing. They retreat alongside their charges when stronger enemies press forward. Their vows forbid loot taken from defeated foes or travelers. Their bare feet mark long journeys across rough roads. They depend upon mobility and goodwill instead of expensive armor.
Combat: 3/2/4/3, cycle (mendicant blessings flagged holy proposal per magic doctrine; never final).
Appearance: barefoot staff-fighter, patched robe, bowl at back. States: staff-circle guard / sweeping trip / stumbled stagger / seated shielding charge. Variants: 2M young walker/older brother; 2F young sister/older mother.

## 59. ENY_RELIC_WARDEN — Relic Warden (fighters + relic-ward prayers, holy flagged proposal)
Relic wardens escort carts carrying old bones and banners between hamlets. They claim no power within relics, only a duty of care. Crowds follow the cart when it travels for seasonal festivals. In combat, they defend the wagon with halberds without advancing. Their loss scatters pilgrims who depended upon their visible protection. Their carts are plain wood, built for travel rather than display. Thieves repeatedly test whether the wardens can defend valuable cargo. Wardens keep close watch on every chest and wheel.

## 60. ENY_PENITENT_BLADE — Penitent Blade (fighters + penitent zeal, holy flagged proposal)
Penitent blades are former criminals working through guilt by serving former victims. They wear ropes at their waists as visible signs of remorse. Folk distrust them first, then praise those who prove steadfast. In combat, they fight fiercely with flails and seek wounds as payment. They spare surrendering foes because mercy demonstrates changed intentions. Their past deeds continue to drive every difficult choice. Formal pardons release most from their chosen lifelong service. Some remain in service because forgiveness never quiets their guilt.
Combat: 5/3/2/2, cycle (penitent zeal flagged holy proposal per magic doctrine; never final).
Appearance: rope-bound fighter, flail, scarred face, plain shirt. States: bowed advance / flail-scourge swing / self-struck stagger / kneeling with grounded flail. Variants: 2M young penitent/scarred elder; 2F hard penitent/older seeker.

## 61. ENY_PALACE_SENTRY — Palace Sentry (generic-only, no courts named)
Palace sentries stand at inner doors of great households, chosen for stillness. They memorize familiar faces, footsteps, and the rhythms of visitors. Residents ignore them until an alarm makes their vigilance necessary. In combat, they hold doorways with spears and shout before striking. They never pursue intruders beyond the posts assigned to them. Their shifts are long, demanding focus during quiet hours. Bribes test their loyalty almost every night of service. A sentry who recognizes danger quickly can protect an entire household.
Combat: 4/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: motionless spearman, plain household cloak unmarked, door-post stance. States: post-stand / spear-level / pushed-back stagger / kneeling at threshold. Variants: 2M young sentry/grey door-warden; 2F hard sentry/older keeper.

## 62. ENY_ESCORT_LANCER — Escort Lancer (generic-only)
Escort lancers ride beside carriages on open roads with bright lances. They drill in pairs to coordinate charges and protective screens. Travelers pay extra for the safety their visible pennants promise. In combat, they charge to scatter ambushes before screening retreats. They abandon baggage when necessary to save people under protection. Their horses are better fed than nearly every other mount. Lameness grounds a lancer and removes their chief advantage. A paired charge can open space for a carriage escape.
Combat: 5/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: mounted lancer, bright lance, carriage-shield, groomed horse. States: carriage-side trot / lance-level charge / unhorsed stagger / grounded shield-ring. Variants: 2M young rider/veteran; 2F hard rider/older leader.

## 63. ENY_VAULT_WARDEN — Vault Warden (generic-only)
Vault wardens guard strong-rooms and granaries of great households using paired keys. They count seals nightly and record any irregularity immediately. Stewards trust their tallies because stock losses threaten everyone nearby. In combat, they fight with short swords among tightly packed chests. They never leave the rooms placed under their direct protection. They may flood or burn stores rather than yield them. Their duplicate keys hang separately to prevent one easy theft. Torture threatens them when intruders seek access to protected stores.
Combat: 4/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: key-hung guard, short sword, seal-board, iron-shod boots. States: chest-lean watch / close cut / key-drop stagger / kneeling over seals. Variants: 2M young keeper/grey master; 2F sharp keeper/older matron.

## 64. ENY_HONOR_BLADE — Honor Blade (generic-only)
Honor blades duel challengers who insult their households under strict counts. They practice daily to maintain exact timing and careful footwork. Their scars are displayed as evidence of past public contests. In combat, they are precise sword duelists seeking disarms before wounds. They refuse group brawls that make honorable counts impossible. Their seconds watch closely for broken rules or unfair interference. Lost duels bring shame upon the households they represent. They value measured skill over the chaos of battlefield fighting.
Combat: 5/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: slim duelist, plain sword, second’s sash, polished boots. States: salute / counted lunge / sash-tear stagger / kneeling yield. Variants: 2M young second/older master; 2F duelist/older mistress.

## 65. ENY_NIGHT_WATCH_CAPTAIN — Night Watch Captain (generic-only)
Night watch captains command household guards after dark, checking posts with lanterns. They know every corridor creak and each guard's expected position. Sleeping residents trust their careful rounds to reveal any threat. In combat, they direct guards with whistles and halberds on stairs. They rally sentries when an alarm breaks ordinary night routine. Their books log every entry, departure, and unexpected disturbance. Missed rounds can doom them when a breach follows unnoticed. Lantern light and disciplined patrols make their authority visible.
Combat: 4/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: lantern-captain, halberd, whistle, round-book. States: stair-stand / halberd-sweep order / book-drop stagger / kneeling directing. Variants: 2M young captain/grey commander; 2F hard captain/older mistress.
