## 1. ENY_GIANT_SPIDER — Giant Web-Spinner (reuse)
Grey web-spinners string cart-rope webs across abandoned barns where thatch has rotted through. They hang motionless for days above livestock pens, feeling every footstep through trembling silk lines. After the fracture emptied toll roads, their webs spread unchecked across granaries and empty watchtowers. Timber-town trappers harvest their silk for bowstrings, paying scarred collectors by the measured skein. Market-town mothers warn children from haylofts where spinners nest thickest in late summer. In battle they drop silently onto shoulders, pinning shields with sticky strands before biting exposed necks. Burning torches drive them backward, for singed spinnerets cannot spin and panic spreads quickly. Villagers burn infested barns each autumn, then rebuild with tarred beams that discourage new anchoring.

## 2. ENY_WEB_RECLUSE — Grey Recluse Spinner
Solitary recluse spinners line dry cellars with low trip-threads that quiver before every strike. They shun sunlit barns entirely, preferring stone foundations where damp keeps their joints supple. After successor tolls thinned rat-catchers, their numbers thickened beneath market-town warehouses and granaries. Warehouse porters test corners with brooms each morning, listening for the faint papery rustle of legs. Ordinary folk dread their shy bite more than wolves, for swelling lingers many painful days afterward. In battle they dart from cracks, nipping ankles swiftly before retreating into shadowed crevices again. Crushed garlic and strong vinegar drive them outward, a trick every cellar-keeper learns early. Burned thatch and lime-washed walls discourage nesting, though vigilant sweeping remains the surest protection.
Combat: 4/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Small ash-grey recluse stands knee-high to humans, violin-marked back gleaming dully in lamplight. Thin legs move without sound, fangs needle-short, abdomen tight as a purse. States: crack-hidden listening stillness / darting ankle-nipping assault / vinegar-flushed scrambling stagger / belly-up curled defeat. Variants (sexes indistinguishable, age-forms only, reason: recluse sexes invisible at combat distance): cellar pale juvenile / warehouse ash elder / violin-dark male-presenting / purse-bellied female-presenting.

## 3. ENY_GIANT_SCORPION — Giant Sand Stinger
Broad sand stingers patrol sun-cracked flats where sparse scrub offers almost no sheltering shade. They dig shallow scrapes beneath thorn bushes, waiting motionless until beetles blunder within reach. After imperial caravans ceased watering southern wells, their burrows multiplied around every neglected trough. Highland-fort outriders prod suspicious sand with lances before allowing thirsty mules to drink deeply. Ordinary drovers fear their tail more than bandits, for even weak venom fells a working mule. In battle they sidle sideways constantly, snapping pincers at shields before arching tails over guards. Heavy boots and thick leggings turn most stings aside, a lesson every caravan guard learns quickly. Villagers flood burrows with brine each spring, then collapse tunnels with ox-drawn stone rollers.
Combat: 5/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Dog-sized amber stinger carries massive pincers and a high-curled segmented tail with dark sting. Armored plates overlap like shingles, legs bristle stiffly, eyes bead black and glassy. States: scrape-hidden motionless ambush / sidling pincer-snapping tail-sting assault / brine-flooded thrashing stagger / overturned twitching defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, size-morphs only, reason: scorpion sexes unreadable in battle): pale sand juvenile / amber flat elder / broad-pincer male-presenting / deep-bellied female-presenting.

## 4. ENY_BURROW_SCORPION — Dune Burrow Scorpion
Deep burrow scorpions engineer branching tunnels beneath dune troughs where trapped moisture lingers longest. They plug entrances with sand pellets each dawn, hiding airflow from keen jackals and hungry birds. After wetland drainage drove mole-catchers northward, their mounds spread across fallow millet fields unchecked. River-port gardeners set buried jars as pit-traps, emptying struggling captives into boiling water daily. Ordinary farmers respect their rat-killing service while cursing every swollen foot suffered at harvest. In battle they erupt from loose soil, seizing boots in crushing pincers before stinging repeatedly downward. Long rakes and probing poles expose tunnels safely, keeping bare hands well clear of entrances. Trampled ground and pastured ducks suppress colonies naturally, for ducks devour juveniles with tireless appetite.
Combat: 4/1/3/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Squat mud-dark burrower stands lower than sand stingers, pincers short and immensely thick. Back caked with soil, tail held low, bristles worn short from constant digging work. States: pellet-plugged tunnel concealment / erupting pincer-crushing sting assault / rake-exposed flailing stagger / caved-in buried defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, caste-forms only, reason: burrower sexes indistinguishable without close inspection): soft tunnel juvenile / mud-caked elder / crusher-claw male-presenting / brood-deep female-presenting.

## 5. ENY_MILLIPEDE_TUNNELLER — Black Millipede Tunneller
Slow black tunnellers chew rotting leaf-mould beneath forested lakeshores where fallen timber lies thickest. They wind through compost in endless armored ribbons, aerating soil that gardeners prize for barley. After charcoal crews abandoned northern pits, their numbers swelled inside damp sawdust piles and ditches. Timber-town sawyers shake boots each morning, for juveniles curl warmly inside unattended footwear overnight. Ordinary folk prize their soil-work while handling them gingerly, for defensive fluid stains skin for days. In battle they coil tightly around ankles, secreting acrid fluid that blisters unprotected skin quickly. Thick grease on boots repels their grip effectively, a trick taught to every apprentice woodcutter. Chickens and lime powder clear infestations swiftly, though gardeners spare outdoor colonies for soil health.
Combat: 3/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Long black ribbon-beast stretches as wide as a forearm and lengthy as a plow line. Countless blunt legs ripple tirelessly, plates gleam oily, head blunt and downturned. States: leaf-mould chewing procession / coiling ankle-gripping fluid assault / grease-slipping uncoiled stagger / split-plate curled defeat. Variants (sexless, segment-forms only, reason: millipede sexes nonexistent for readable variants): short red juvenile coil / long black elder ribbon / mossy compost crawler / sawdust pale roller.

## 6. ENY_GARDEN_MILLIPEDE — Striped Garden Millipede
Striped garden millipedes patrol compost heaps behind market-town houses where vegetable peelings rot deeply. They march nightly along mortar joints, cleaning mould that would otherwise sicken stored grain supplies. After siege gardens replaced flower courts, their quiet service earned tolerance from even fastidious cooks. City wives sweep them gently into jars, releasing workers back onto garden beds after inspections. Ordinary children race them along tiles, learning patience from their slow unstoppable marching columns. In battle they swarm over feet in numbers, releasing sour clouds that sting eyes and throats. Vinegar-soaked rags block doorways reliably, turning marching columns aside without any killing needed. Flooded gutters drown colonies each storm season, yet survivors rebuild heaps within a single week.
Combat: 2/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Short chestnut-striped roller curls readily into a tiled ball nearly fist-sized in hands. Plates banded cream and rust, legs fine as eyelashes, head tucked invisibly when threatened. States: mortar-joint marching procession / swarming sour-cloud release assault / vinegar-turned scattering stagger / balled unmoving defeat. Variants (sexless, pattern-forms only, reason: garden millipedes show no readable sexes): cream juvenile roller / rust-banded elder marcher / tile-pale compost sweeper / gutter-dark storm survivor.
