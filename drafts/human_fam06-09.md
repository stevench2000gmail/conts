## 31. ENY_KNIFE_ASSASSIN — Knife Assassin (generic-only)

In open combat they are deadly in first rushes with paired daggers, weaker in long stands. They aim for quick kills then flight through crowds. They never claim masters or houses, only prices. Their blades are plain to avoid tracing.
