# Humanoid Families 11-12 — Ratfolk (5) + Naga (2, ex-snakefolk), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 48. ENY_RATFOLK_SNITCH — Ratfolk Snitch
Whisker-twitching scouts infest granary walls where spilled grain sweetens every dark breath. Their bands breed quickly inside tiled culverts the empire built and successor towns forgot. They trade stolen manifests for cheese-rinds and sheltered nesting corners. Merchants secretly pay them to learn rivals’ hidden stores. They fear seasoned mousers far more than sleepy human watchmen. In battle they dart between legs with short knives, overwhelming isolated victims through numbers. They scatter screaming the moment their chieftains fall. Their musky droppings betray every regular route.
Combat: 3/1/5/1, always_physical.
Appearance: child-sized rat-headed skulker with short knife and grain-sack, whiskers trembling, tail balancing behind human knees. Patchy fur, red eyes. States: wall-niche crouch / darting pack-stab / tail-yanked stagger / piled whimpering surrender. Variants: 2M young squeaker / grey whisperer; 2F breeding dam / quick sister.

## 49. ENY_RATFOLK_BLADE — Ratfolk Blade
Scarred tunnel fighters guard nesting warrens with rusted swords longer than themselves. Their bands raid cellars along streets the night watch patrols thinly. They learned pack tactics watching stray dogs surround butchers’ boys. Butchers hang their pelts on doors as warnings. They respect only the biggest teeth. In battle they surge with rusted blades, trading wounds without hesitation. They break when fire appears. Their nests stink of tallow and fear.
Combat: 4/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: battle-scarred rat warrior with rusted sword and sewn-hide armor, standing waist-high to humans, tail lashing. One ear torn. States: tunnel-mouth guard / surging blade-swarm / fire-panic scatter stagger / kneeling with dropped sword. Variants: 2M young fighter / scarred guard; 2F hard dam-fighter / older brood-guard.

## 50. ENY_RATFOLK_PLAGUE — Ratfolk Plague Carrier (disease as mundane filth, no new magic)
Filth-caked carriers spread spoilage through wells they bathe in deliberately. Their bands weaponize rot where old sewers collapsed. They understand contagion through observed custom, never through sorcery. Villagers burn their bedding on discovery. They trade antidotes they distrust themselves. In battle they hurl dung-pots then stab with filthy daggers, weakening foes through disgust. They wear cloth masks against their own stench. Boiled water defeats them.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, cycle (filth-then-stab, mundane).
Appearance: pox-marked carrier with dung-pot bandolier and stained mask, fur matted, flies circling human-height clouds. Oozing sores. States: pot-ready hover / dung-toss dagger rush / coughing stagger / slumped over broken pots. Variants: 2M young mixer / pocked master; 2F sharp mixer / older dam.

## 51. ENY_RATFOLK_ENGINEER — Ratfolk Engineer
Clever wire-twisters build deadfall traps and cranked dart-throwers from stolen bell parts. Their devices mimic old artillery in miniature without gunpowder sorcery. Millers pay them to kill competitors’ wheels. They love clicking mechanisms above all food. In battle they trigger spring-snares then swarm with wrenches. Their machines jam constantly. Oil and spare wire sustain them. Smashed workshops end them.
Combat: 3/1/5/2, always_physical.
Appearance: gear-laden tinkerer with wrench-club and wire coils, goggles of bottle-glass, standing knee-high to humans. Grease-smeared whiskers. States: device-crouch calibration / snare-spring wrench rush / jammed-device stagger / seated among broken gears. Variants: 2M young twister / grey inventor; 2F quick assembler / older keeper.

## 52. ENY_RATFOLK_BROODMOTHER — Ratfolk Broodmother (band-level only)
Vast breeding mothers anchor warrens, birthing litters that replenish every lost raid. Their hunger dictates every foray into granaries and kitchens. Bands starve without their steady flow. They remember no history beyond full bellies. In battle they defend nesting chambers with crushing bulk and teeth, never pursuing beyond. Their death dissolves warrens into quarreling packs. Rival mothers absorb survivors. Warmth and grain sustain them.
Combat: 5/1/2/4, always_physical.
Appearance: enormous bloated mother with crushing paws and swollen belly, filling tunnel mouths, dwarfs human children. Milk-heavy, blind with protectiveness. States: nest-blocking bulk / crushing bite sweep / pup-loss frenzy stagger / collapsed nursing surrender. Variants: 4F young mother / prime dam / grey dam / barren aunt (female-led reproduction, males 0 with reason: breeding hierarchy).

## 53. ENY_NAGA_CONSTRICTOR — Naga Constrictor (ex-snakefolk)
Heavy-bodied constrictor bands coil in steaming marsh reeds beyond the last farm ditches. Their scaled torsos rise human-high above muscular tails that crush wild pigs. They learned ambush patience before successor roads existed. Fisherfolk trade smoked eels for safe passage through their waters. They worship no gods, only warm stones. In battle they grapple and squeeze lone victims, avoiding formed spear lines. They release dead prey slowly. Their shed skins mark territories.
Combat: 5/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: human-torsoed constrictor with green-brown scales and crushing tail, rearing taller than humans, tongue flickering. Broad shoulders. States: reed-coil ambush / grapple-squeeze / spear-prodded release stagger / uncoiled gasping surrender. Variants: 2M young coil / scarred crusher; 2F brooding dam / older shedder.

## 54. ENY_NAGA_AMBUSHER — Naga Ambusher
Smaller mottled ambushers haunt forest-floor litter where sun breaks in speckles. Their bands coordinate silent surrounds with tail-taps. They take deer and unwary travelers alike. Charcoal-burners dread their leaf-rustle. They collect warm stones. In battle they spring from cover with short spears, constricting stragglers. They abandon hunts against fire. Their nests hold clutch-eggs.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: mottled small constrictor with short spear and leaf-litter cloak, lower than humans when coiled, eyes amber. Quick tongue. States: litter-hidden coil / springing spear-grapple / fire-driven release stagger / coiled shielding eggs surrender. Variants: 2M young striker / grey waiter; 2F nesting dam / older hunter.
