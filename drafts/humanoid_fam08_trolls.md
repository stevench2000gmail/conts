# Humanoid Family 08 — Trolls (5, outside-society bands, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 36. ENY_TROLL_BRIDGE — Bridge Troll
Bridge trolls are solitary hulks who claim fords and demand meat-tolls, wading where toll brutes merely threaten. They stand a head over ogres, moss-backed and river-stinking, kin to stone-folk without their speech. After the fracture left bridges unguarded they simply moved in. Drovers pay in goats. They count poorly and forget debts. In battle they sweep with whole pines, damming streams with bodies. They hate fire the way all night-folk do, though no magic burns them here.
Combat: 8/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: moss-backed giant with pine-trunk sweep and ford-mud caked, looming over humans like a boulder, eyes small. Dripping beard. States: ford-stand block / trunk-sweep flood / fire-shy stagger / seated in shallows. Variants: 2M young wader / grey mossback; 2F hard wader / older dam.

## 37. ENY_TROLL_HILL — Hill Troll
Hill trolls roam high pastures in pairs, lifting sheep and shepherds alike. They learn paths the way bears do. Flocks vanish whole. Shepherds fire the hillsides to drive them. In battle they hurl tors and grapple, slow but certain. They nap after kills. Their dens are thorn-choked. Winter hunger emboldens them.
Combat: 7/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: pasture giant with tor-stones and shepherd’s-crook trophies, sun-cracked hide, loping gait. States: ridge-lurk / tor-hurl grapple / sun-dazzle stagger / sprawled on flock-remains. Variants: 2M young roamer / scarred taker; 2F hard ewe-lifter / older dam.

## 38. ENY_TROLL_CAVE — Cave Troll
Cave trolls haunt quarry deeps and cellar warrens, blind in daylight. They hear heartbeats through stone. Miners seal shafts against them. They eat anything warm. In battle they erupt from dark with claw and weight, terrible in tunnels, helpless blinking in sun. Their eyes are pale coins. Lanterns hold them. Their guano enriches fungi.
Combat: 8/1/1/2, always_physical.
Appearance: pale giant with clawed hands and quarry-dust, stooped under open sky, ears twitching. States: dark-eruption rush / claw-rake crush / sun-blind stagger / groping seated. Variants: 2M young digger / blind elder; 2F hard mother / older brood-keeper.

## 39. ENY_TROLL_RIVER — River Troll
River trolls lurk reed-beds to drag boats, cousins to bridge kin but swimmers. They learn currents. Barge crews dread still reaches. They trade with smugglers for rope. In battle they capsize then drown-grapple, weak ashore. Their reed-nests float. Nets tangle them. Drought strands them.
Combat: 7/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: reed-crowned swimmer with hook-claws and boat-plank club, green-black, low in water. States: reed-float stalk / capsize-drag / beached flop stagger / netted gasping. Variants: 2M young diver / grey floater; 2F hard diver / older nest-keeper.

## 40. ENY_TROLL_HAG — Troll Hag (band-level only)
Hags are eldest troll females holding scattered kin by food-lore and threats. They brew bone-broths that stretch feasts. Young trolls bring tribute. They claim no rule beyond the marsh-edge. In battle they scold and swing iron pots, shielding young behind bulk. Their curses are noise, never magic. Their fall disperses kin for seasons. Their broths heal no wounds, only hunger.
Combat: 6/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: vast elder female with iron pot and stirring oar, warts like knots, voice like landslides. States: pot-guard scold / oar-sweep / tripped stagger / seated stirring. Variants: 4F old hag / daughter / sister / grey aunt (female-led line, males absent with reason: sons eat separately).
