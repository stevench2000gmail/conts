## 57. ENY_FOREST_OWL — Brown Forest Owl
Silent brown owls haunt forested lakeshores where voles rustle beneath rotting leaf litter nightly. They glide without whisper between mossy trunks patiently, striking before prey hears any wingbeat. After charcoal burners thinned underbrush for kilns, their hunting lanes widened along new rides. Riders lash lanterns low on saddles nightly, avoiding low stoops that snatch caps from heads. Ordinary timber folk welcome vole control warmly while fearing omens hooted before each death. In battle they swoop at lanterns suddenly, clawing faces with silent extensions of talons. Hooded lamps and broad hats prevent injuries effectively during night work in occupied woods. Martens and hard frosts thin nests yearly, though hollow oaks shelter breeders through winters.
Combat: 3/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Broad brown owl looms cat-sized, facial disks pale and heart-shaped around black eyes. Wings fringed silently, talons needle-sharp, hoot deep and carrying across still water. States: trunk-gliding silent patrol / swooping face-clawing lantern assault / lamp-hooded circling stagger / marten-raided fallen defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via bulk, reason: owl females outweigh males): fluffy owlet squeaker / brown lakeshore hunter / smaller tiercel male / massive brooding female.
