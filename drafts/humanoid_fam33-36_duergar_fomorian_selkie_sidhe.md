## 140. ENY_DUERGAR_GREY — Grey Duergar
Ash-grey duergar bands work lightless quarry depths far below exile dwarves. Their sullen fighters wield mattocks against everything living. They remember imagined slights for decades. They enslave stray kobolds ruthlessly. In battle they fight silently in tight files with short spears, never routing while leaders stand. They poison wells spitefully. Their grey skin camouflages in ash.
Combat: 5/1/3/4, always_physical. (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: Ash-grey low fighter carries a mattock-spear and slave-chain, eyes red-rimmed, beard sparse and lank. Sunken cheeks. States: lightless-file silent advance / spear-thrust holding assault / leader-fallen routing stagger / chain-offering sullen surrender. Variants: 2M young drudge / grey taskmaster; 2F hard driver / older keeper.

## 141. ENY_DUERGAR_TASKMASTER — Duergar Taskmaster (band-level only)
Scar-lashed taskmasters drive mixed slave gangs with whip and tally. Their feared bands strip mines bare. They count lashes meticulously. They sell surplus slaves. In battle they flog allies forward while striking foes with weighted whips, inspiring terror. Their death frees gangs instantly. Rival taskmasters absorb survivors.
Combat: 5/1/4/5, always_physical. (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: Scar-lashed driver carries a weighted whip and tally-board, voice cracking like stone, bulk squat and menacing. Branded hands. States: lash-cracking driving command / whip-striking terrifying assault / tally-smashed bewildered stagger / whip-yielding bitter surrender. Variants: 2M young lasher / scarred master; 2F hard mistress / older counter.

## 142. ENY_FOMORIAN_BRUTE — Fomorian Brute
Single-eyed brute fomorians wade ashore through crashing surf at bleak dawns. Their twisted frames combine human limbs with goat heads. They raid coastal hamlets for cattle. Fisherfolk bar doors at their bellows. They demand milk tribute. In battle they sweep with driftwood clubs, goring with mismatched horns. They retreat to sea when burned.
Combat: 7/1/2/2, always_physical. (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: Twisted goat-headed giant carries a driftwood club and tide-pool armor, single eye glaring, limbs mismatched and powerful. Salt-crusted. States: surf-wading bellowing advance / club-sweep goring assault / fire-driven seaward stagger / tribute-accepting snorting surrender. Variants: 2M young wader / scarred raider; 2F hard sea-wife / older keeper.

## 143. ENY_FOMORIAN_EYE — Fomorian Eye Tyrant (band-level only, no death-gaze as final)
Baleful-eyed tyrant fomorians direct raiding crews with hateful glaring stares rumored lethal falsely. Their actual power is uncannily precise rock-throwing, flagged never as final sorcery. Subordinate bands obey subtle eye-twitch commands instantly. They extort coastal villages for cattle regularly. In battle they hurl heavy tors with devastating accuracy while bellowing orders continuously, shielding young behind immense bulk. Their blinded fall routs crews instantly. Their deliberate blinking signals prepared retreats.
Combat: 7/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: Massive single-eyed tyrant carries tor-stones and a command shell-horn, gaze baleful, bulk barnacled. Voice foghorn-deep. States: stare-directed silent command / tor-hurling bellowing assault / eye-struck screaming stagger / tribute-accepting yielding surrender. Variants: 2M young eye / scarred tyrant; 2F hard lady / older keeper.

## 144. ENY_SELKIE_SHORE — Shore Selkie (fixed)
Soft-eyed shore selkies shed heavy sealskins upon moonlit skerries in order to dance as humans. Their gentle bands net silver salmon peacefully alongside cooperative fisherfolk every dawn. They marry lonely sailors in many persistent old stories. They hide precious skins extremely carefully beneath tidal rocks. In battle they defend stolen skins ferociously with sharp gutting knives, fleeing transformation theft desperately. They ransom captured skins afterward with polished pearls. Their moonlit skerry dances entrance lonely watchers completely. Stolen skins enslave them utterly until returned.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Soft-eyed seal-woman carries a carefully folded sealskin and sharp gutting knife, movements liquid and graceful, hair black and sleek always. Human form appears graceful and vulnerable ashore.
States: skerry-dancing moonlit vigil / skin-defending knife assault / skin-seized despairing stagger / pearl-offering tearful surrender. Variants: 2M dark husband / grey bull; 2F young maid / grey skin-keeper.

## 145. ENY_SELKIE_DEEP — Deep Selkie (fixed)
Grey-bodied deep selkies hunt cold cod far beyond sight of familiar land. Their completely silent bands follow migrating whale pods across deep oceans. They navigate accurately by low song-currents only. They trade beachcombed amber to smiths for precious iron. In battle they ram fragile boats with immense bulk in seal form, then stab survivors with bone daggers ashore afterwards. They cannot remain landlocked for very long periods. Their remote deep skerries remain permanently unmapped by pilots. Warm shallow waters sicken them rapidly.
Combat: 4/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Grey-bodied powerful selkie carries sharp bone daggers and a heavy amber pouch, bulk solid and scarred, eyes large and dark always. Seal form appears massive beside small boats.
States: migration-following silent patrol / boat-ramming dagger assault / landlocked gasping stagger / amber-offering deep surrender. Variants: 2M old bull / scarred diver; 2F hard cow / older singer.

## 146. ENY_SIDHE_WANDERER — Sidhe Wanderer (fixed)
Silver-tongued sidhe wanderers walk lonely twilight roads between ancient grassy mounds. Their luminous bands trade riddling news gladly for shallow cream bowls. They remember solemn oaths sworn long before successor kings ruled. Frightened villagers bar heavy doors tightly during their glowing processions. They adore haunting music far above cold silver. They fear cracked iron bells above all weapons. In battle they dazzle victims with polished mirrored shields before cutting with thin swords, vanishing into cold mists afterwards completely. They ransom captured travelers afterward in exchange for new songs.
Combat: 4/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Luminous pale wanderer carries a polished mirrored shield and slender thin sword, beauty profoundly unsettling, movements impossibly graceful always. Long hair shines brightly like captured moonlight constantly.
States: twilight-road procession vigil / dazzling sword-cutting assault / bell-rung scattering stagger / song-accepting bowing surrender. Variants: 2M pale rider / grey whisperer; 2F bright lady / older keeper.

## 147. ENY_SIDHE_MOUND — Sidhe Mound Keeper (band-level only, fixed)
Moss-crowned mound keepers guard rounded green hill doors opening only on feast nights. Their solemn bands collect regular tolls of fresh milk and wild honey. They remember long-buried kings beneath every hill. They punish oath-breakers cruelly with eternally losing forest ways. In battle they mislead intruders with floating lantern lights before spearing from dark mounds, never pursuing beyond grassy hills. Their heavy doors grind shut exactly at dawn. Their golden honey stores attract hungry bears constantly. A deliberately drained mound kills them very slowly.
Combat: 4/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: Moss-crowned solemn keeper carries a tall lantern-pole and heavy hill-key, presence deeply ancient, eyes star-filled and distant. Heavy green cloak drags damply behind.
States: hill-door guarding vigil / lantern-misleading spearing assault / dawn-caught blinking stagger / honey-accepting yielding surrender. Variants: 2M old doorkeeper / grey toller; 2F stern lady / older keeper.
