# Humanoid Family 01 — Gnomes (5, outside-society bands, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 1. ENY_GNOME_SCRAPPER — Gnome Scrapper
Burrow bands of gnome scrappers scratch a living in scrub hills where thin soil defeats plows but hides warrens. They stand half a man’s height, broad-nosed and mud-caked, built low for tunnel work the empire once taxed. After the fracture left border farms unguarded, these bands traded winter labor for lifted grain and learned to fight farmers’ dogs. Ordinary folk laugh at their waddle until a whole pack boils from a ditch with picks, for numbers make them bold. They share food by strict pebble-count and exile thieves by burying them neck-deep till dawn, a justice travelers find crueler than whipping. In open battle they rush ankles with picks and hammers, brave in packs of six and cowardly alone, breaking the moment a shield-wall holds. They know every rabbit-run for miles and move camps each season, leaving swept burrows patrols find empty. Cold drives them down to storehouses, and only blocked burrows and fed dogs thin them.
Combat: might 3 / magic 1 / intelligence 4 / leadership 2, always_physical.
Appearance: half-height burrower with pick-hammer and mud-wraps, big-nosed and round-bellied beside a human child, knuckles calloused from digging. Small boots, pebble-pouch, tunnel-dirt ingrained. States: burrow-crouch ambush / ankle-rush pick-swing / hurled-back sprawl / piled surrender with raised hands. Variants: 2M young digger / grey elder; 2F hard mother / young lass.

## 2. ENY_GNOME_TINKER — Gnome Tinker
Tinker gnomes push whole workshops on their backs along farm lanes, mending kettles that successor smiths charge too dear to fix. Their people learned travelling trade when imperial roads still ran, and kept to it when tolls tripled, for a moving camp pays no hearth-tax. Villagers welcome the tune of their little hammers and count spoons after, loving the skill and fearing the lift. They trade sharpened knives for meals and sleep under barrows, telling tunnel-tales that borrow Frieren’s wandering warmth and Warhammer’s grumbling craft without claiming either world’s magic. Their oil-flasks and soot-gourds are mundane tools, and any “flash” is thrown ash, flagged never as final magic. When cornered they blind with soot then stab with awls, brave only behind smoke. Rain rusts their stock and ruins routes, forcing them to winter near mills. A mended plow buys them passage where spears would not.
Combat: 2/1/5/2, cycle (soot-then-stab, mundane).
Appearance: pack-laden mender with tool-bandolier and soot-goggles pushed high, smaller than a human youth, fingers black with polish. Barbed awl, spare handles, barrow-straps. States: pack-kneel offer / soot-toss awl-stab / pack-drop stagger / seated on tools with raised palms. Variants: 2M young apprentice / grey master; 2F quick lass / older dame.

## 3. ENY_GNOME_SLINGER — Gnome Slinger
Slinger bands hold scrub rocks above flock-runs, hired by shepherds to drop crows and thieves at a hundred paces. Their long slings descend from herder craft older than any successor levy, and their river-stone pouches never empty. Drovers pay in cheese to keep them posted, for a whistling stone warns before it kills. Like Tolkien’s hill-folk they love high places and hate to come down, fighting only from cover. When rushed they drop slings and flee down goat-paths humans cannot follow, useless in melee. Wind spoils their aim and wet straps snap, grounding them. Their challenge-whistles carry farther than horns. A broken sling ends a slinger’s season.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: rock-perched small figure with long sling and stone-pouch, scrub-cloak mottled grey-green, sharp eyes beside a human’s knee. Bare toes curled on stone, spare cords at wrist. States: rock-aim poise / stone-loose whirl / rock-fall tumble / grounded with empty pouch upturned. Variants: 2M young guard / grey eye; 2F keen lass / older keeper.

## 4. ENY_GNOME_BADGER_RIDER — Badger Rider
Badger riders are gnome outriders bonded for life to huge burrowing badgers, patrolling warren lands at dusk. The pairing began when pups were dug from flood-collapsed setts and hand-fed, growing into mounts strong enough to unhorse a man. Strangers laugh at the striped rush until claws open a horse’s belly. Rider and beast fight as one creature, charging low and clawing, and riders lose all heart if mounts fall. Their setts are fortress warrens no patrol will enter. Hunger makes them bold enough to raid storehouses. Meat tribute buys quiet roads. A fed sett stays hidden for seasons.
Combat: 4/1/3/3 (rider+beast as one fighter), always_physical.
Appearance: spear-carrying gnome astride a badger tall as a wolf, war-painted stripes matching the beast, bridle of braided roots. Rider’s legs barely reach the beast’s ribs. States: mounted patrol trot / badger-charge spear-drive / unseated sprawl / grounded clutching reins. Variants: 2M young rider / scarred master; 2F hard rider / older breeder.

## 5. ENY_GNOME_ELDER — Gnome Elder
Elders too old to dig keep tunnel-tales and settle burrow quarrels by counted stones. They remember droughts, soldiers, and the year the imperial tax-men stopped coming, and their memory is the band’s only archive. Bands obey their whistle-codes and share by their pebbles. In battle they stand behind young ranks shaking rattle-staffs, their weak thrusts mattering less than voices that hold lines. Their fall scatters packs into every ditch. They teach which farms feed and which set dogs, a lore travelers would pay for. Winter kills most, and a heard elder steadies a line better than a shield.
Combat: 2/1/5/5, always_physical.
Appearance: grey stooped gnome with tale-staff and counted-stone pouch, wrinkles deep as plow-furrows, wrapped in layered burrow-blankets. Smaller for age, eyes milky but loud. States: staff-raised order / short thrust / bowed stagger / seated giving orders. Variants: 2M old digger / blind singer; 2F old mother / tale-keeper.
