# The Thirty-Six Fallen — Nine Angelic Orders Roster

**Status:** Draft / proposal for review  
**Structure:** 9 former angelic Orders × 4 Fallen each = 36

## Core Rules

- The Thirty-Six are fallen celestial beings organized according to the **nine angelic Orders**: Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones, Dominions, Virtues, Powers, Principalities, Archangels, and Angels.
- Each Order contains exactly **four Fallen**.
- Jewish/Christian candidates must have an actual basis in **fallen, rebellious, adversarial, Watcher, or related demonic/angelic traditions**. Faithful angels such as Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael are not reclassified as fallen simply to fill the roster.
- Candidates from polytheistic traditions are prominent gods/deities whom the game's fictional cosmology interprets as fallen celestial beings later remembered and worshipped as gods.
- Supreme/ultimate creator deities and historically attested humans later deified are generally excluded.
- Candidates must not duplicate the traditional **72 Ars Goetia spirits**, including obvious spelling variants or historical-name collisions representing substantially the same entity.
- The mythological identities and their placement are fictional game lore, not claims about the real-world religions.
- The angelic Order determines the nature and scope of the original celestial office. Individual combat power does not always follow hierarchy.
- The central corruption principle is:

> **A Fallen does not abandon their original virtue. They pursue it after losing the virtues that once restrained it.**

## Monotheistic vs. Polytheistic Candidate Rule

### Jewish / Christian / Enochic

Candidates from monotheistic traditions must already have a meaningful basis as **fallen, rebellious, adversarial, Watcher, or related figures** in the source traditions.

The setting should **not** take faithful figures such as Michael, Gabriel, or Raphael and arbitrarily make them Fallen simply because their traditional angelic roles fit one of the nine Orders.

Current candidates:

- Lucifer / Satan
- Samael
- Azazel
- Sariel
- Kokabiel
- Gadreel

The exact historical status, spellings, and traditions surrounding **Samael, Sariel, Kokabiel, and Gadreel** should receive a dedicated source-validation pass before final canonization.

### Polytheistic Traditions

The remaining candidates are prominent supernatural gods/deities.

Within the game's fictional cosmology, mortal civilizations encountered or remembered these fallen celestial beings and interpreted them as gods. Their traditional mythological domains preserve distorted memories of their original angelic offices.

For example:

- **Odin** was formerly a **Cherub** concerned with wisdom.
- **Nuwa** was formerly a **Throne** concerned with restoration and cosmic stability.
- **Indra** was formerly a **Dominion** concerned with command.
- **Susanoo** was formerly a **Virtue** governing controlled natural force.
- **Xuanwu** was formerly a **Power** concerned with celestial defense.
- **Amaterasu** was formerly a **Principality** concerned with the ordering of mortal society.
- **Inari** was formerly an **Angel** concerned with particular communities and provision.

## Hierarchy and Power

The numbering follows former angelic hierarchy:

1–4 — Seraphim  
5–8 — Cherubim  
9–12 — Thrones  
13–16 — Dominions  
17–20 — Virtues  
21–24 — Powers  
25–28 — Principalities  
29–32 — Archangels  
33–36 — Angels

A lower number generally indicates greater **original celestial precedence and scope**, but it is **not a strict combat-power ranking**.

A fallen Virtue associated with storms, fire, plague, or thunder may possess greater immediate destructive power than a higher-ranked being whose office concerned wisdom, judgment, or governance.

## Ars Goetia Exclusion Rule

The Thirty-Six must remain separate from the traditional **72 spirits of the Ars Goetia**.

Reject:

1. exact Goetic names;
2. alternate spellings/transliterations of the same Goetic spirit;
3. older divine names when the Goetic name substantially derives from or represents that same historical entity;
4. renamed versions intended merely to avoid an obvious collision.

Examples of names to avoid include **Bael/Baal, Amon/Amun, Astaroth/Astarte, Asmoday/Asmodeus, and Belial**.

**Belial is therefore not part of this roster.**

## Canon Validation Still Required

Before promoting this roster from proposal to canon:

1. Source-check **Samael, Sariel, Kokabiel, and Gadreel** carefully against Jewish/Christian/Enochic traditions.
2. Run a final exact-name, variant-name, and historical-identity comparison against all **72 Ars Goetia spirits**.
3. Confirm whether **Lucifer and Satan are permanently one entity** in game canon.
4. Decide whether the numerical position inside each group of four represents meaningful internal seniority.
5. Decide whether the mythological names are the Fallen's actual celestial names or names later assigned to them by mortal cultures.
6. Decide the exact relationship between the **Thirty-Six Fallen** and the **72 Goetic demons**.
