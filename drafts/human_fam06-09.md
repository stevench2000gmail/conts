# Human Families 06-09 — hired swords / assassins / ninjas / militia (20 entries, LOCKED, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. Assassins generic-only (no guilds). Ninjas Japanese role-names, no clans. Taxonomy Human. All magic 1.

## 26. ENY_HIRED_BLADE — Hired Blade (reuse)
Hired blades are practiced fighters whose discipline sets them above ordinary outlaws. They sell their swords to caravans, households, and threatened traveling parties. Daily drills keep their blades oiled and their footwork reliably sharp. Employers trust their steady hands when danger gathers around guarded gates. Villagers sometimes pool scarce coin to hire them against predatory gangs. In combat, they remain balanced and patient, parrying before measured ripostes. They withdraw when contracts end, rather than fleeing from sudden panic. Their reputation determines their wage, and broken contracts ruin future hires.
Combat: reuse ENY_HIRED_BLADE (5/1/5/4).
Appearance: clean sellsword, arming sword, buckler, maintained harness. States: balanced guard / measured cut / parry-shake stagger / kneeling with grounded sword. Variants: 2M young sword/weathered veteran; 2F duelist/older captain.

## 27. ENY_DUELING_TUTOR — Dueling Tutor
Dueling tutors teach blade manners to merchants' sons and guard officers. They take temporary hires between lessons to maintain their own standing. Their polished style comes from school-yards rather than harsh battlefields. Proud students boast loudly about the masters who taught them. In combat, tutors are precise and showy, preferring disarms over deaths. They may challenge travelers who publicly insult their favored pupils. Their costly lessons often earn more than ordinary hired work. A humiliating lost duel can empty an instructor's entire school.
Combat: 4/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: slim instructor, foil and padded jack, chalked hands. States: salute stance / lunge-flourish / robe-tear stagger / seated with broken foil. Variants: 2M young graduate/older master; 2F sharp mistress/older matron.

## 28. ENY_BODYGUARD — Bodyguard
Bodyguards shadow wealthy travelers and paymasters, paid to bleed before clients. They are chosen for loyalty and vigilance rather than flashy swordplay. Their watchful eyes study crowds more carefully than individual blades. Households sleep more easily when a bodyguard stands beside doors. In combat, they interpose bodies and shields before striking clear space. They retreat alongside their charges rather than chasing defeated attackers. Every scar marks a life they successfully kept from harm. A dead charge can end a bodyguard's entire trade.
Combat: 5/1/3/4, always_physical.
Appearance: broad shield-bearer, tower shield, short sword, watchful scan. States: charge-cover stance / shield-bash / staggered shield-drop / kneeling over fallen charge. Variants: 2M young shield/older oathman; 2F stern guard/older protector.

## 29. ENY_ROAD_WARDEN — Road Warden
Road wardens are licensed blades patrolling trade stretches for toll cooperatives. They serve as part guard and part tax collector. Long patrols teach them every ambush bend along their assigned roads. Drovers gladly pay when familiar wardens display their protective pennants. In combat, they hold the road-center steadily with practical halberds. They pursue raiders with resolve but spare those who pay. Their recognizable license marks are copied and forged surprisingly often. Without wardens, thugs reclaim exposed roads within only several days.
Combat: 4/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: pennanted halberdier, road-cloak, license badge, horn. States: road-center plant / halberd-sweep / horn-drop stagger / seated at milepost. Variants: 2M young warden/grey patrol; 2F hard rider/older keeper.

## 30. ENY_RETIREDSERGEANT — Retired Sergeant
Retired sergeants sell drill and muscle after years of military service. They shape local militias and guard stored goods through dangerous seasons. Their seasoned shouts can still form disciplined lines from frightened rabble. Villagers pool funds to retain one throughout the difficult winter. In combat, they direct allies while striking short, hard, and cunningly. Their firm presence often anchors formations that would otherwise quickly rout. Their stories teach recruits more lasting lessons than their battered swords. Age slows their legs, but experience keeps their eyes sharp.
Combat: 4/1/5/5, always_physical.
Appearance: grey veteran, short blade, drill-stick, medal-stripped coat. States: stick-raised order / short chop / knee-buckle stagger / seated giving orders. Variants: 2M lame youth-trained/bald elder; 2F hard widow-sergeant/older drillmistress.

## 31. ENY_KNIFE_ASSASSIN — Knife Assassin (generic-only)
Knife assassins kill for coin where crowded quarters conceal a sudden blade. They accept single contracts, taking payment before and after each killing. They ask no reasons, only prices, timings, and useful descriptions. Folk fear their quiet approach more than soldiers' noisy patrols. The law hangs them without mercy, so they work alone and masked. In open combat, paired daggers make their first rushes especially deadly. They seek quick kills before escaping through the surrounding crowds. Their plain blades reveal neither masters, houses, nor easily traced origins.
Combat: 5/1/5/2, always_physical.
Appearance: plain-cloaked killer, paired daggers, masked wrap, soft boots. States: crowd-blend stalk / twin-stab rush / exposed stagger / crumpled with dropped masks. Variants: 2M young blade/pale veteran; 2F quiet girl/older widow.

## 32. ENY_GARROTE — Garrotter (generic-only)
Garrotters strangle chosen marks in dark lanes without spilling visible blood. They favor debt disputes and inheritance quarrels where silence pays best. Night walkers fear footsteps approaching from behind more than open threats. Garrotters carry wire in belt loops disguised as common tools. In combat, they grapple from behind and struggle face-to-face. If seized, they thrash, bite, and fight desperately for escape. They commonly work as pairs, with one holding and one pulling. Their distinctive wire marks condemn them when authorities catch them.
Combat: 4/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: wiry strangler, wire-loops at belt, heavy cuffs, hooded. States: trailing stalk / wire-pull grapple / thrown-off stagger / pinned with cut wire. Variants: 2M thin youth/thick elder; 2F small woman/older cleaner.

## 33. ENY_POISONER — Poisoner (generic-only)
Poisoners sell powders and draughts to clients who fear steel. They work from herb stalls and dye shops without drawing notice. Their knowledge distinguishes gut-wrenching herbs from valuable healing ones. Cooks and cup-bearers dread their hidden trade more than most. Proven poisoners lose their shops when the law takes action. In combat, they rely on prepared darts and rotating mundane dusts. Their advantage is readiness, which fades quickly after close engagement. They carry antidotes for themselves and seldom share them freely.
Combat: 3/1/5/2, cycle (trick-dust rotation, mundane).
Appearance: stained herbalist, bandolier of vials, cloth mask, careful gloves. States: vial-ready hover / dust-toss then stab / coughing stagger / slumped over spilled vials. Variants: 2M young mixer/pockmarked master; 2F sharp mixer/older dyer.

## 34. ENY_CROSSBOW_KILLER — Crossbow Killer (generic-only)
Crossbow killers shoot from upper windows and barn lofts at chosen marks. Many are failed soldiers or hunters preferring distance to personal daggers. Crowds scatter quickly when the first prepared bolt strikes nearby. They own no uniforms, relying instead upon rented rooms and cover. In combat, they fire once before drawing short swords when rushed. Their real craft is the prepared shot, never prolonged melee. They abandon crossbows readily when escape demands speed and mobility. Shuttered windows and zigzagging streets make their attacks much harder.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical (crossbow as physical).
Appearance: hooded marksman, folding crossbow, bolt-case, upper-window cloak. States: loft-aim / bolt-loose / rushed sword-draw stagger / toppled with empty case. Variants: 2M young hunter/grey shot; 2F keen lass/older widow.

## 35. ENY_BATHHOUSE_BLADE — Bathhouse Blade (generic-only)
Bathhouse blades strike where victims bathe or sleep unarmed and exposed. They pose as attendants, quietly learning habits over several patient days. Hosts fear them deeply because hospitality can become a concealed ambush. Their short blades remain hidden inside ordinary folded towels. In combat, they move quickly against unarmored foes with single daggers. Ready guards expose their frailty and quickly limit their opportunities. They flee through undressed crowds, using confusion as protective cover. Locked inner doors and personal guards effectively block their routines.
Combat: 4/1/5/1, always_physical.
Appearance: attendant-garbed killer, towel-fold dagger, damp hair, soft sandals. States: towel-offer stalk / close stab / seized-wrist stagger / wrapped in torn sheets defeated. Variants: 2M young attendant/older keeper; 2F maid/older matron.

## 36. ENY_SHINOBI — Shinobi (male-only exception: male role tradition, 4 male variants)
Shinobi are hired infiltrators who scale walls and watch roads for pay. They train upon cliffs and rooftops, valuing silence above brute strength. Households dread the faint scratches heard near shuttered windows at night. They carry hooks and short blades while avoiding open confrontations. In combat, they evade foes and stab from behind. They are weaker during sustained stands against prepared opponents. When pressed, they escape across roofs using practiced climbing routes. Dogs and loose gravel reliably expose their otherwise silent movements.
Combat: 4/1/5/2, always_physical.
Appearance: dark-wrapped male climber, hook-rope, short blade; lean, masked. States: wall-crouch / back-stab dart / tangled stagger / netted seated. Variants: 4M young creeper / scarred scaler / broad diver / grey watcher.

## 37. ENY_KUNOICHI — Kunoichi (female-only exception: female role tradition, 4 female variants)
Kunoichi pose as servants and errand women to overhear valuable secrets. They learn poison herbs and message codes alongside practiced smiles. Hosts most fear the familiar hands they once trusted completely. Hairpin daggers and powder gourds remain concealed within their clothing. In combat, they strike dazed foes after mundane ash flashes. Once exposed, they are frail against alert and ready opponents. They vanish into crowds of women when pursuit becomes dangerous. Their fans hide signals, while locked inner rooms block access.
Combat: 3/1/5/3, cycle (flash-then-strike, mundane).
Appearance: servant-garbed woman, fan, hairpin dagger, gourd at sash. States: serving hover / flash-slash / seized-wrist stagger / wrapped seated. Variants: 4F young maid / dancer / older matron / grey go-between.

## 38. ENY_GENIN — Genin (young low rank, all youthful visuals)
Genin are apprentice operatives sent on errands, watches, and first thefts. They are chosen young for their speed and small frames. They run messages through guarded streets using memorized lantern codes. Ordinary folk often mistake these youthful runners for beggars. In combat, they throw sparks and run rather than fight. Their quick legs are useful, but their blades remain unreliable. They memorize patrol routes, and sweets can purchase useful information. A caught genin often cries and reveals everything they know.
Combat: 2/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: youthful runner, ragged wraps, signal strips, quick feet; visibly teen. States: loitering errand / spark-toss flee / tripped sprawl / held with strips. Variants: 2M young boys / 2F young girls — all under twenty look.

## 39. ENY_CHUNIN — Chunin (older high rank, all mature visuals, higher stats)
Chunin lead cells and plan difficult entries after many experienced seasons. They read guards and weather, timing actions that others execute. Employers pay more for their judgment than their personal blades. In combat, they strike short and hard while directing genin. Their steady decisions anchor retreats that might otherwise become panicked flights. Their scars provide lessons that younger operatives cannot easily learn. Age may slow their legs, but never their watchful eyes. Losing a chunin can collapse an entire operative cell.
Combat: 5/1/5/4, always_physical.
Appearance: mature operative, quality wraps, command fan, grey at temples; visibly thirty-plus. States: fan-raised order / directed cut / winded stagger / seated giving orders. Variants: 2M older field-lead / grey planner; 2F older handler / grey coordinator — all mature look.

## 40. ENY_TEPPO_SHINOBI — Teppo Shinobi (matchlock ninja, flagged mundane firearm tech, no new magic)
Teppo shinobi shoot from lofts and field blinds using long matchlock tubes. This late craft was learned from foreign traders, without enchantment. Their single roaring shot can decide an ambush before blades meet. Crowds scatter quickly when smoke suddenly blooms around their positions. They rent upper rooms and abandon heavy tubes while fleeing. In combat, they rely upon one devastating shot before weak swordplay. Rain and damp weather ruin the powder they require. Shuttered windows and zigzagging approaches effectively foil their preparations.
Combat: 4/1/3/2, always_physical (matchlock as physical, flagged tech proposal).
Appearance: hooded marksman, long tube, powder-horn, rest-stick, smoke-stained sleeves. States: loft-aim / roar-loose / rushed sword stagger / toppled with empty horn. Variants: 2M young gunner / older shot; 2F keen gunner / older loader.

## 41. ENY_VILLAGE_MILITIA — Village Militia
Village militias are farmers and craftspeople mustered with spears and bells. They defend barns and homes when thieves threaten nearby families. They drill on rest days beneath retired soldiers' stern instruction. Neighbors trust these familiar defenders more than distant garrison troops. In combat, they bravely hold hedge lines despite raw experience. Heavy rushes can break them, though warning bells rally them quickly. Their strength comes from numbers and intimate knowledge of home ground. At dawn, they disperse again to their ordinary daily work.
Combat: 3/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: homespun coat, spear and hide shield, harvest boots. States: bell-line stand / spear-poke / line-break stagger / seated with grounded spear. Variants: 2M young plowman/older headman; 2F hard wife/young daughter.

## 42. ENY_TOWN_WATCHMAN — Town Watchman
Watchmen walk night rounds carrying lanterns and halberds through market towns. They know familiar drunks and troublesome doors by name. Folk mock them during calm days, yet miss them afterward. In combat, they stand steady with halberds and call early. They hold narrow lanes rather than attempting open battlefield maneuvers. Their whistles summon heavier help when trouble exceeds their numbers. Poor pay leaves some watchmen vulnerable to tempting bribes. Honest watchmen nevertheless keep regular rounds through difficult weather.
Combat: 3/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: lantern-halberdier, town cloak, whistle-cord, badge-disk. States: lantern-swing patrol / halberd-sweep / whistle-drop stagger / seated at post. Variants: 2M young recruit/grey roundsman; 2F hard watcher/older keeper.

## 43. ENY_HEDGE_WARDEN — Hedge Warden
Hedge wardens guard field boundaries and orchards from poachers and boars. They work as solitary trackers carrying bows through tangled vegetation. Farmers feed them to keep watchful eyes facing outward. In combat, they snipe first before falling back with knives. They understand snares better than most formally trained soldiers do. Their dogs help track intruders and warn of approaching danger. Long winters thin their regular rounds across exposed boundaries. Briar scratches and muddy boots mark their persistent outdoor work.
Combat: 3/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: green-cloaked tracker, short bow, snare-wire, briar-scratched. States: hedge-crouch / snap-shot / knife-draw stagger / kneeling with empty quiver. Variants: 2M young tracker/older gamekeeper; 2F swift lass/older warden.

## 44. ENY_FIRE_BRIGADE — Fire Brigade
Fire brigades are bucket and hook crews who answer sudden fires. During riots or raids, they double as practical axe militia. Masons and roofers fill their ranks, making them strong on ladders. Settlements love them after fires but fear their strength during brawls. In combat, they swing axes and hooks with labor hardened force. Their experience balancing above streets lets them hold rooftops well. Brigade bells rally nearby militias whenever immediate reinforcements are needed. Dry seasons keep every crew alert, organized, and ready.
Combat: 4/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: soot-stained crew, fire-axe, hook-pole, leather helm. States: ladder-brace / axe-chop / smoke-cough stagger / seated on buckets. Variants: 2M young roofer/broad mason; 2F hard carrier/older captain.

## 45. ENY_RIVER_PATROL — River Patrol
River patrols pole skiffs along channels checking toll tokens and smugglers. They know hidden reeds, shifting shallows, and reliable crossing places. Boatmen either respect their authority or attempt to bribe them. In combat, they hook and shove effectively from unstable boats. Ashore, their waterborne training leaves them notably weaker than opponents. They defend waterways, not broad land routes or fortified walls. Their narrow skiffs serve as mobile forts among familiar channels. Strong storms beach patrols and leave waterways temporarily unguarded.
Combat: 3/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: oilskin patroller, boat-hook, token-board, bare feet. States: skiff-pole stance / hook-pull / overboard stagger / beached seated. Variants: 2M young poler/older cox; 2F river lass/older inspector.
