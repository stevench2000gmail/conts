# Humanoid Families 37-38 — Minotaurs (2) + Centaurs (2), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free, every sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 148. ENY_MINOTAUR_MAZE — Maze Minotaur
Horned maze minotaurs haunt crumbling quarry labyrinths beyond patrolled roads. Their brooding bands memorize every twisting passage without error. They challenge lost intruders to ritual single combat underground. Quarry crews leave meat offerings near familiar tunnel mouths. They collect every labyrinth map with obsessive, suspicious care. Open plains leave them exposed, frightened, and deeply confused. Massive horns gore opponents before stone mauls crush them. They spare defeated challengers who kneel immediately and surrender.
Combat: 7/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Horned towering bull-man carries a stone maul and labyrinth chart, nostrils flaring widely, shoulders mountainous and scarred. Hooves strike sparks. States: labyrinth-pacing brooding vigil / goring maul-crushing assault / plain-stranded bellowing stagger / offering-accepting snorting surrender. Variants: 2M young bull / scarred maze-lord; 2F hard cow / older keeper.

## 149. ENY_MINOTAUR_FORGE — Forge Minotaur
Soot-horned forge minotaurs tend charcoal pits inside foothill caves. Their patient bands hammer crude bronze with deliberate pride. They understand bellows rhythms through long practice beside furnaces. They trade finished blades for grain from nearby farmers. White-hot bars swing from tongs and scald through shields. Quenching floods terrify them because water ruins their furnaces. Black slag heaps mark their territory beside smoking cave mouths. Their horned smiths defend every pit with unyielding strength.
Combat: 7/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Soot-horned bulky smith carries white-hot bars with massive tongs, apron charred black, arms corded hugely. Heat shimmering constantly. States: pit-tending hammering stance / bar-swinging scalding assault / quenched hissing stagger / grain-accepting grunting surrender. Variants: 2M young stoker / sooty master; 2F hard smith / older keeper.

## 150. ENY_CENTAUR_PLAINS — Plains Centaur
Four-hooved plains centaurs roam windswept valley grasslands in small herds. Their proud bands race approaching storms for pure joy. They remember old migration routes through each changing season. Farmers trade grain for warnings about dangerous roaming threats. They despise fences that break their traditional running paths. Storm feathers collected after gales decorate their braided manes. They loose short bows while galloping beyond spear reach. Fallen foes risk merciless trampling beneath their thundering hooves.
Combat: 5/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Four-hooved tall centaur carries short bows and a feather bandolier, human torso rising above dappled flanks, mane braided tightly. Hooves thunder. States: grassland-roaming racing patrol / galloping bow-wheeling assault / fenced trapped panicking stagger / grain-accepting nodding surrender. Variants: 2M young runner / scarred racer; 2F swift mare / older keeper.

## 151. ENY_CENTAUR_WOOD — Wood Centaur
Bark-armored wood centaurs patrol dense forest borders where carts move slowly. Their wary bands escort travelers after collecting reasonable tolls. They understand timber prices and watch every loaded wagon. Clear-cuts provoke their anger and lasting woodland grief. They block narrow trails with their considerable bulk. Long spears strike from behind their living wooden armor. Deep woods shelter their retreat when trails become unsafe. Their bark armor regrows each year after winter storms.
Combat: 5/1/3/4, always_physical.
Appearance: Bark-armored stocky centaur carries a long spear and toll-board, flanks scarred by branches, torso thick and weathered. Leaves tangled. States: border-trail blocking stance / bulk-shoving spear assault / clear-cut grieving stagger / toll-accepting escorting surrender. Variants: 2M young guard / mossy elder; 2F hard mare / older warden.
