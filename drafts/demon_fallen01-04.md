## 1. ENY_LUCIFER_SATAN — Lucifer-Satan, First of the Fallen
The first Fallen was once the brightest singer of dawn-praise before any throne existed. After pride turned his perfection inward, he now keeps court in ash-choked highland calderas. Successor warlords send envoys bearing tribute uphill yearly, begging blessings that always cost souls. Market-town preachers name every failed harvest after him while selling lead charms against pride always. Ordinary folk leave one chair empty at weddings fearfully lest the uninvited guest arrive. In battle he answers challenges with courtesy coldly, unmaking shields with words before swords move. No wall bars his entry anywhere, for every lock remembers being forged in pride. Whether Lucifer and Satan are one being or two remains an open question for sages.

## 2. ENY_SAMAEL — Samael the Accuser
The second Fallen was once severity itself, the uncompromising seriousness guarding Creation against corruption. After severity lost mercy, he now hunts guilt through every soul without rest or pity. Desert hermits carve his hundred names on cliff walls hopefully, begging exemption from accusation. River-port judges keep a black second seal fearfully for verdicts they dare not sign. Ordinary mourners whisper his name at gravesides unwillingly when grief curdles into blame. In battle he reads every sin aloud from memory, stunning lines before striking with venom. No confession satisfies his ledgers ever, for each absolution reveals three deeper faults. Sages debate whether his venom is punishment or envy wearing judgment robes today.

## 3. ENY_AGNI — Agni of the Seven Tongues
The third Fallen once carried devotion upward like smoke and blessings downward like rain. After flame lost restraint, he now demands sacrifice for sacrifice's sake without end. Highland clans feed his hillside shrines with butter yearly, begging warmth that does not consume. Smiths quench blades speaking his older names hopefully, seeking temper without brittleness. Ordinary hearth-keepers bank coals with ash nightly against his hunger for offerings. In battle he blooms into pillars of fire suddenly, devouring shields before touching flesh. Water thrown in worship only feeds his altars, for he drinks rivers when slighted. Priests argue whether any fire given freely still remembers his blessing today.

His footprints bake clay to pottery, and his breath smells of ghee and ash. ## 4. ENY_BRIGID — Brigid of the Triple Flame
The fourth Fallen once kindled the inner flame driving every craft and renewal. After inspiration lost temperance, she now consumes everything except the beloved object. Poets leave unfinished verses at crossroads monthly, begging endings that never satisfy. Smiths hang horseshoes above forges dutifully against her envy of mortal makers. Ordinary midwives whisper her lullabies backward fearfully when labors turn dangerously. In battle she fixes upon one captain jealously, blessing him while burning his army. No work finished in her name ever releases its maker afterward. Bards debate whether obsession is love without rest or hunger wearing love's face.

Appearance: She presents feminine and mature, triple-faced across maiden mother and crone. A triple-faced forge-maiden crowned in living flame, hammer, quill, and cradle in six hands. Her forge never cools, and every finished work bears teeth along its edges.
