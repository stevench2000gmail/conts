Drovers dread their giggling approach. They collect cracked bones. They fear disciplined spear lines. In battle they circle laughing with serrated spears, breaking isolated victims. They flee fire.

## 92. ENY_GNOLL_BONEBREAKER — Gnoll Bonebreaker
Massive bonebreaker gnolls crush steaming marrow bones with heavy stone mauls. Their hungry bands camp stubbornly at fresh kill sites for days. They remember hidden waterholes across drought years. They trade cured hides for iron spearheads. In battle they smash raised shields with pulverizing mauls, opening holes for packmates. They gorge themselves mid-fight when blood flows. Their powerful teeth crack thick femurs easily. Resident lions contest every carcass fiercely.
Combat: 6/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: massive crusher with stone maul and marrow-bowl, jaws massive, shoulders humped. States: carcass-guard stance / maul-shatter smash / lion-driven stagger / marrow-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young crusher / scarred breaker; 2F hard crusher / older keeper.

## 93. ENY_GNOLL_HUNTER — Gnoll Hunter
Lean hunter gnolls run down swift gazelles across shimmering open flats. Their disciplined bands coordinate exhausting relay pursuits for hours. They read shifting winds with lifted noses. In battle they exhaust foes with harassing javelin casts, never closing too early. They hamstring stragglers ruthlessly. Their legendary endurance outlasts cavalry horses. Sharp thorn fields lame them quickly.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical. (Note: 7-sentence sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: lean runner with javelin-bundle and wind-veil, ribs showing, legs tireless. States: relay-trot stalk / harassing javelin rain / thorn-lamed stagger / scent-lost sitting surrender. Variants: 2M young runner / grey relator; 2F swift dam / older finder.

## 94. ENY_GNOLL_SPEAKER — Gnoll Speaker (no true magic, tricks only)
Cackling speaker gnolls conduct rattling rites over carefully cracked skull piles. Their supposed power is infectious laughter and acrid rot-smoke, never true magic. Bands fight visibly emboldened beneath howling. In battle they howl quavering calls before striking with knobbed staves. Their sudden fall quiets packs instantly. Their masks are hollowed hyena skulls. Cold rain stills their rites completely.
Combat: 3/1/4/4, cycle (howl-then-strike, mundane).
Appearance: skull-masked howler with knobbed staff and smoke-gourds, voice cracking, eyes rolling. States: skull-circle howl / staff-crack lunge / mask-shatter stagger / seated silent surrender. Variants: 2M young howler / scarred cackler; 2F hard caller / older mother.

## 95. ENY_GNOLL_PACKLORD — Gnoll Packlord (band-level only)
Dominant packlord gnolls hold many quarreling families through repeated victories. Their chilling giggles silence disputes instantly. In battle they duel enemy champions personally with paired crescent axes. Their sudden death splits packs into feuding splinters. Their bone-piled dens serve as thrones. Stronger neighboring lords absorb leaderless survivors. A consistently full hunting ground keeps fragile peace.
Combat: 6/1/3/4, always_physical.
Appearance: paired-axe lord with bone-throne furs and many-scarred hide, larger than packmates, laugh commanding. States: giggling challenge stance / paired-axe duel / axe-sunder stagger / throne-yielding surrender. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred lord; 2F hard lady / older matriarch.
