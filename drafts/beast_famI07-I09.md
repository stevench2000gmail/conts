# Beast Families I07-I09 — Mud-lobsters (2) + Ditch leeches (2) + Hive wasps (2), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Beast. Mud-lobsters burrow vs river-crab shore split. Leeches sexless. Wasps caste-forms only.

## 13. ENY_MUD_LOBSTER — Black Mud Lobster
Sullen black lobsters dig elbow-deep burrows along tidal mudflats where river meets delta silt. They cap tunnels with neat mud chimneys each evening, guarding watery shafts from gulls and otters. After embankment crews abandoned southern sluices, their chimney fields spread across fallow clam beds. Clam diggers probe chimneys with short sticks each morning, flipping defenders into wicker baskets quickly. Ordinary coastal folk steam catches with sour herbs, stretching thin winter stews with sweet tail meat. In battle they back into burrows stubbornly, snapping protruding claws at probing hands and boots. Poured brine flushes occupants upward rapidly, a method every mudflat child learns before harvest age. Staked nets and turned eels thin colonies naturally, though drained flats collapse burrows permanently afterward.
Combat: 4/1/1/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Stout black lobster bulks forearm-long, one crusher claw massive and one cutter narrow. Shell rough as bark, antennae sweep constantly, tail fans powerfully for backward escape bursts. States: chimney-capped burrow guard / backing claw-snapping defense assault / brine-flushed exposed stagger / flipped tail-curled defeat. Variants (2M/2F readable via claw bulk, reason: lobster sexes differ in claw and tail width): soft juvenile crawler / black chimney elder male / broad-tail berried female / pale moultling.

## 14. ENY_BURROW_LOBSTER — Pale Burrow Lobster
Blind pale lobsters tunnel far beneath freshwater marshes where peat stains every pool tea-brown. They navigate by sweeping antennae constantly, sharing flooded galleries with eels and patient catfish. After mill dams stilled lower currents, their collapsed sinkholes pocked pastures across the wetland fringe. Ditch riders mark fresh sinkholes with willow wands, warning night patrols away from treacherous ground. Ordinary peat-cutters dread cave-ins more than claws, for mired oxen cost dearly to recover. In battle they surge from broken banks suddenly, seizing leggings with surprising sideways lunges. Boiled linseed smeared on boots deters gripping effectively, buying time to retreat from banks. Limers drain side galleries each autumn, collapsing networks while sparing main breeding chambers deliberately.
Combat: 3/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Eyeless cream lobster feels alien, claws equal-sized and needle-tipped for soft peat digging. Shell thin and flexible, antennae enormously long, legs paddle clumsily on open ground. States: peat-gallery hidden waiting / surging legging-seizing lunge assault / linseed-slicked fumbling stagger / stranded paddle-limp defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, antenna-forms only, reason: blind burrower sexes invisible in darkness): translucent juvenile drifter / cream gallery elder / long-antenna male-presenting / deep-bodied female-presenting.

## 15. ENY_DITCH_LEECH — Fat Ditch Leech
Fat black leeches carpet stagnant ditches where cattle drink sluggishly through hot summer afternoons. They writhe toward warmth unerringly, fastening onto fetlocks before herders notice any itching bite. After drainage taxes lapsed during succession wars, their numbers choked every lowland watercourse and pond. Leech-gatherers wade bare-legged deliberately each dawn, harvesting engorged specimens for barber-surgeons and healers. Ordinary villagers sell surplus jars for coppers while cursing bloody ankles suffered at harvest. In battle they drop from reeds collectively, seeking exposed skin with blind relentless hunger. Salt pouches and tight leggings prevent most attachments effectively during wetland crossings always. Ducks and drained ditches suppress swarms naturally, though healers protect managed breeding pools carefully.
Combat: 2/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Slick black leech swells thumb-thick when fed, segmented body rippling with muscular waves. Suckers gape visibly at both ends, skin shines oily, movement silent through shallow water. States: ditch-carpeting waiting writhe / dropping skin-seeking suction assault / salt-shrivelled thrashing stagger / dried curled defeat. Variants (sexless, engorgement-forms only, reason: leeches are hermaphroditic without readable sexes): thread-thin hungry juvenile / fat black engorged elder / reed-green unfed drifter / salt-scarred pool keeper.

## 16. ENY_BLOODMARSH_LEECH — Red Bloodmarsh Leech
Crimson marsh leeches haunt steaming hot springs where mineral water stains surrounding rocks rust-red. They cluster beneath floating algae mats patiently, sensing hoofbeats through trembling water long before arrival. After bathhouse taxes closed hillside pools, their undisturbed colonies thickened across abandoned stone basins. Bath attendants skim colonies with fine nets hourly, keeping paying soak-pools clear for guests. Ordinary pilgrims seek controlled bites for aching joints while fearing wild swarms in open marsh. In battle they latch onto calves quickly, swelling visibly as they drain warmth and strength. Ash paste smeared thickly on skin blocks suckers reliably, a recipe every marsh guide carries. Stocked carp devour juveniles eagerly, keeping managed springs balanced without total extermination ever.
Combat: 3/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Bright crimson leech glows alarmingly against green algae, shorter and thicker than ditch cousins. Stripes pulse darker when feeding, suckers ringed pale, body contracts violently when seized. States: algae-mat hidden clustering / latching calf-draining swell assault / ash-blocked writhing stagger / scraped salted defeat. Variants (sexless, spring-forms only, reason: bloodmarsh leeches show no distinct sexes): orange spring juvenile / crimson mat elder / rust-banded basin drifter / pale mineral stunted keeper.

## 17. ENY_HIVE_WASP — Amber Hive Wasp
Organized amber wasps raise papery fortresses beneath market-town eaves where roof heat lingers longest. They patrol fixed lanes aggressively, memorizing every laundry line and butcher scrap route nearby. After orchard walls crumbled during grain riots, their foraging parties stripped fallen fruit within hours. Orchard keepers hang smoked fish decoys downhill, drawing hungry scouts away from harvest ladders. Ordinary housewives dread autumn most, for bold foragers invade kitchens seeking meat and honey. In battle they dive at eyes collectively, stinging cheeks and hands with fiery repeated jabs. Wet blankets and smoke pots break attacks effectively, calming defenders enough for careful nest removal. Winter frost kills queens reliably except in sheltered lofts, where careful smoke clears survivors early.
Combat: 3/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Sleek amber wasp grows thumb-long, narrow waist pinched tightly between thorax and striped abdomen. Wings blur glassily, antennae twitch constantly, sting slides visibly when threatened greatly. States: lane-patrolling hovering guard / diving eye-seeking sting assault / smoke-calmed groggy stagger / wing-torn grounded defeat. Variants (caste-forms only, reason: wasp sexes collapse into worker/queen castes for readability): small worker skirmisher / broad queen mother / dark drone drifter / winter-pale loft survivor.

## 18. ENY_PAPER_WASP — Grey Paper Wasp
Grey paper wasps glue delicate nests inside hollow oaks where deep forest meets valley pasture edges. They harvest weathered fence wood patiently, chewing fibres into thin layered papery walls each spring. After timber tolls priced oak beyond reach, their abandoned nests became tinder prized by charcoal burners. Burners smoke colonies at dusk carefully, harvesting empty combs after workers settle for night. Ordinary woodcutters mark active trees with chalk warnings, steering apprentices toward safer timber stands. In battle they spiral upward confusingly, then slash downward at noses with irritating shallow stings. Mud smeared on exposed skin deters stings surprisingly well during forest work every summer. Woodpeckers and hard frost thin colonies yearly, though sheltered hollows preserve breeding queens reliably.
Combat: 2/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Slender grey wasp appears ghostly beside amber cousins, longer legs dangling in flight visibly. Nest papery grey and layered, comb hexagonal and precise, queen larger with coppery sheen. States: hollow-oak nest tending / spiralling nose-slashing sting assault / mud-foiled milling stagger / frost-killed papery defeat. Variants (caste-forms only, reason: paper wasp sexes read as castes in battle): pale spring chewer / grey autumn guard / coppery queen mother / frost-grey hollow keeper.
