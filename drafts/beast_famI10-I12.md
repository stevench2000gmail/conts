## 19. ENY_MOUND_ANT — Red Mound Ant
Industrious red ants raise gravel mounds across high plateau pastures where short grass hides seeds. They file along scented highways constantly, hauling crumbs heavier than their own small bodies. After shepherds abandoned summer folds during border raids, their mounds choked watering paths and gates. Drovers pour boiling water into nest mouths each evening, keeping night folds clear for lambs. Ordinary herders respect their seed-cleaning service while cursing fiery bites suffered at shearing. In battle they swarm boots collectively, climbing inward toward warmth with needle-pricked biting jaws. Greased leather gaiters block climbers effectively, while stamped ash circles confuse trailing scent lines. Anteaters and hard frost thin mounds yearly, though deep queens survive to rebuild every spring.
Combat: 2/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Small rust-red ant swarms numerously, segmented bodies armored brightly like polished seeds. Jaws lift visibly, antennae wave constantly, mound gravel excavated grain by tiny grain. States: highway-filing forage procession / swarming boot-climbing bite assault / ash-circled milling stagger / flooded collapsed defeat. Variants (caste-forms only, reason: ant sexes collapse into worker/queen castes): tiny worker hauler / winged prince drifter / broad queen mother / dark winter bunker-keeper.

## 20. ENY_FIRE_MOUND_ANT — Cinder Mound Ant
Dusky cinder ants colonize warm ash slopes where volcanic soil stays heated through cold nights. They harvest dead moths blown uphill daily, storing charred wings inside insulated nursery chambers. After ore-town slag was dumped freely downhill, their glassy mounds glittered across spoil heaps. Slag pickers test ground with iron rods hourly, avoiding colonies that swarm boots without warning. Ordinary miners trade ant-cleared crumbs to chickens while dreading blistered feet during night shifts. In battle they erupt from disturbed cinders suddenly, stinging ankles with burning venomous jabs repeatedly. Cold clay smeared on boots soothes stings somewhat while discouraging further climbing attacks effectively. Flooded meltwater drowns shallow nests each thaw, yet deep chambers preserve queens for rebuilding.
Combat: 3/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Soot-dark ant glints faintly orange under sunlight, stouter than red plateau cousins considerably. Sting extends visibly when threatened, legs grip cinders strongly, mound glassy and heat-hardened. States: slope-harvesting scattered foraging / erupting ankle-stinging swarm assault / clay-blocked hesitant stagger / drowned collapsed defeat. Variants (caste-forms only, reason: cinder ant sexes read as worker/queen castes): ash-grey juvenile hauler / soot-dark elder guard / orange-glint queen mother / slag-pale heap drifter.

## 21. ENY_LOCUST_SWARM — Green Locust Swarm
Ravenous green swarms darken green valley skies where barley ripens thickest before harvest week. They descend as rattling curtains suddenly, stripping leaves, thatch, and linen within bare hours. After granary reserves were seized for armies, their famines drove villagers to eat locusts themselves. Hungry families roast catches with salt eagerly, turning plague into bitter survival feasts each autumn. Ordinary farmers dread distant humming more than wolves, for one swarm ruins a whole year. In battle they cloud around heads densely, battering eyes with wings and biting exposed necks. Smoke fires and drenched blankets break clouds effectively, allowing runners to reach sheltered barns. Ducks, children with nets, and hard rain thin swarms, though eggs persist in soil for years.
Combat: 3/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Sleek green locust flies strongly, hind legs folded like springs beneath veined wings. Jaws grind constantly, eyes bulge widely, swarm hum deepens before every sudden descent. States: sky-darkening humming approach / clouding head-battering bite assault / smoke-scattered breaking stagger / rain-beaten grounded defeat. Variants (swarm-forms only, reason: locust sexes invisible inside swarms): bright green solitary hopper / dark gregarious flier / yellow migrant leader / brown winter egg-keeper.

## 22. ENY_DELTA_LOCUST — Grey Delta Locust
Grey delta locusts breed inside river delta reedbeds where flood silt renews grasses every spring. They hop among stilts patiently, nibbling thatch that villagers must replace before rainy seasons. After fish weirs trapped floating eggs downstream, their hatchings choked ferry landings with hopping mats. Ferrymen sweep decks with birch brooms hourly, shoveling struggling masses back into hungry water. Ordinary reed-cutters boil catches for ducks while patching roofs against persistent nibbling damage. In battle they spring toward warmth collectively, striking shins with spurred hind legs repeatedly. Oiled canvas wrapped around legs prevents gripping effectively during crossings through infested reeds. Herons and drained breeding pools suppress numbers locally, though floods always restore colonies eventually.
Combat: 2/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: Mottled grey hopper blends perfectly with reed stems, smaller and duller than valley cousins. Hind spurs hook sharply, wings short but serviceable, eyes striped faintly gold and brown. States: reedbed hopping nibble / springing shin-striking swarm assault / oil-slicked sliding stagger / heron-picked scattered defeat. Variants (swarm-forms only, reason: delta locust sexes indistinguishable in reedbeds): mottled reed juvenile / grey silt elder / gold-eyed thatch nibbler / flood-pale ferry drifter.

## 23. ENY_GRAVE_FLY — Blue Grave Fly
Metallic blue flies gather thickly wherever unburied offal lies near tanner yards and shambles. They dart in nervous loops constantly, tasting rot that spreads fever through crowded alleys quickly. After butcher guilds bribed inspectors freely, their swarms blackened market-town awnings every summer noon. Apprentices fan stalls with horsehair switches hourly, keeping meat barely clear enough for sale. Ordinary cooks dread their touch more than mice, for tainted joints sicken whole households afterward. In battle they cloud faces persistently, crawling toward eyes and mouths with filthy tickling legs. Vinegar washes and covered dishes prevent contamination reliably during warm months every year. Swallows and cold snaps thin swarms dramatically, though warm middens preserve breeding pockets through winter.
Combat: 1/1/3/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Small metallic blue fly flashes brilliantly, body compact and bristled with probing mouthparts. Wings beat invisibly fast, eyes redden hugely, buzz sharpens unpleasantly near human ears. States: offal-circling tasting hover / clouding face-crawling harassment assault / vinegar-driven dispersing stagger / frost-killed silent defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, glint-forms only, reason: grave fly sexes differ only by eye spacing): green juvenile dart / blue shambles elder / red-eyed market haunter / dull winter midden keeper.

## 24. ENY_CARRION_FLY — Grey Carrion Fly
Dull grey flies haunt cave mouths where bats roost thickly above guano-stained stone floors. They breed inside droppings rapidly, hatching maggots that cleanse roosts better than any broom. After hermits began selling guano fertilizer downhill, their disturbed clouds plagued timber-town gardens seasonally. Gardeners hang sticky tar strings densely, trapping clouds before vegetables become specked and unsellable. Ordinary miners swallow mouthfuls unwillingly while cursing every coughing fit suffered on night shifts. In battle they funnel toward lanterns collectively, choking flames with bodies and dimming light dangerously. Wet moss masks hung over mouths filter swarms effectively during crossings through infested passages. Spiders and strong drafts thin clouds naturally, though undisturbed guano always rebuilds numbers quickly.
Combat: 1/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Tiny grey fly appears drab beside blue cousins, hairy body dusted with pale guano powder. Eyes dull maroon, wings smudged faintly, maggots creamy and tirelessly wriggling below. States: guano-hovering breeding cloud / lantern-choking light-dimming assault / draft-scattered thinning stagger / tar-trapped silent defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, dust-forms only, reason: carrion fly sexes invisible without lenses): pale guano juvenile / grey cave elder / tar-specked garden drifter / moss-dusted mine haunter.
