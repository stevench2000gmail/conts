# Humanoid Family 25 — Lizard marsh stalkers (5, high-count, separate from kobolds; distinct from Naga and croc-folk), Phase 1, no JSON — EXPANDED full 8x8+
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 106. ENY_LIZARD_STALKER — Marsh Stalker
Scaled marsh stalkers glide silently through reed-channels where dark water hides every approach. Their patient bands ambush thirsty drinking herds at purple dusk. They learned statue-stillness from basking crocodiles over generations. Nervous fisherfolk trade fresh fish for warning clicks. They collect smooth sun-warmed stones compulsively. They fear sudden cold snaps greatly. In battle they erupt roaring with barbed spears, dragging screaming victims into deep water. They abandon hunts quickly against prepared fire.
Combat: 5/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: scaled green stalker with barbed spear and stone-pouch, tail balancing, eyes lidless, human-tall and low. States: channel-float ambush / erupting spear-drag / cold-stiffened stagger / nest-retreat surrender. Variants: 2M young floater / scarred patient; 2F nesting dam / older keeper.

## 107. ENY_LIZARD_DARTER — Marsh Darter
Slim darters sprint tirelessly across sucking mudflats on two balancing legs, herding silver fish into woven nets. Their coordinated bands drive schools with waving tail-flags. They race each other for sport at dawn. They trade salted roe to villagers for iron. In battle they encircle victims with whistling light javelins, never standing to trade blows. They tire visibly after long pursuits. Their bright flags signal every turn. Mired black mud traps them helplessly.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: slim two-legged darter with light javelins and tail-flag, lean and quick, smaller than humans. States: mudflat sprint circle / encircling javelin rain / mired flail stagger / flag-lowered surrender. Variants: 2M young runner / grey driver; 2F swift dam / older netter.

## 108. ENY_LIZARD_DIVER — Marsh Diver
Deep divers hold their breath for long dark minutes beneath black still water. Their silent bands pry blue mussels and lost iron from sunken wrecks. They navigate lightless depths purely by touch. Grateful divers trade rare salvage for lamp oil. They fear barbed hooks above all. In battle they capsize rocking skiffs from below, drowning panicked swimmers mercilessly. They cannot fight effectively ashore. Their sunken caches glitter with old coins.
Combat: 4/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: broad-chested diver with salvage net and bone knife, gill-slits fluttering, eyes large. States: black-water hover / capsize-drag drown / beached gasp stagger / cache-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young diver / grey lug; 2F hard diver / older keeper.

## 109. ENY_LIZARD_SUNSEER — Sunseer (no true magic)
Basking sunseers read coming weather in glossy scale-sheen and towering cloud color. Their dozing counsel picks seasonal migrations for the bands. Basking bands coil respectfully around their warm rocks. They loudly claim sun-favor, though power is observation only. They anoint departing scouts with sacred mud. In battle they chant rasping hymns then jab with fire-hardened staves, rallying young fighters. Their sudden fall scatters basking bands instantly. Their sacred basking rocks draw pilgrims.
Combat: 3/1/5/4, cycle (chant-then-jab, mundane).
Appearance: sun-bleached basker with staff and mud-bowl, scales pale, posture sprawled. States: rock-bask chant / staff-jab rally / cloud-covered falter stagger / mud-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young basker / grey reader; 2F warm dam / older seer.

## 110. ENY_LIZARD_MIRELORD — Mirelord (band-level only)
Scarred mirelord lizards hold flooded territories through repeated crushing victories. Their deep bellows silence disputes among younger stalkers instantly. Subordinate bands follow their deepest croaks obediently. In battle they crush challengers personally with massive jaws and sweeping tails, dueling champions while bands clash. Their sudden death splits territories into warring reaches. Their sinking mud-thrones require yearly rebuilding. A consistently full marsh keeps fragile peace.
Combat: 7/1/2/4, always_physical.
Appearance: massive scarred stalker with jaw-scars and mud-throne mound, bulk dwarfing kin, bellow shaking reeds. States: bellow-challenge stance / jaw-tail crush duel / jaw-wired stagger / throne-yielding surrender. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred lord; 2F hard lady / older dam.
