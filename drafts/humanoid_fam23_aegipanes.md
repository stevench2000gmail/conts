# Humanoid Family 23 — Aegipanes (5, high-count, highland goat-folk herders; split from Satyr raiders), Phase 1, no JSON — EXPANDED full 8x8+
Rule: city-free, every sketch sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 96. ENY_AEGIPAN_HERDER — Aegipan Herder
Shaggy highland herders drive sure-footed goats across windswept stone terraces above the clouds. Their goatish legs climb crumbling scree where human herders fall to their deaths. They carve plaintive bone flutes during long lonely watches. Lowland farmers trade precious grain for their sharp cheese. They despise every fence and locked gate. In battle they butt intruders with horned heads and hook with heavy crooks, defending bleating herds fiercely. They retreat swiftly uphill where armored pursuers stumble helplessly. Their circular stone folds dot high pastures like crowns.
Combat: 4/1/3/2, always_physical.
Appearance: shaggy goat-legged herder with crook and bone flute, horns curling, beard braided, human-torsoed above woolly flanks. States: terrace-lean watch / horn-butt crook-hook / fence-tangled stagger / fold-retreat surrender. Variants: 2M young herd-boy / grey goatherd; 2F hard herd-maid / older keeper.

## 97. ENY_AEGIPAN_PIPER — Aegipan Piper
Hollow-horn pipers play echoing tunes that carry clearly across deep misty valleys. Their scattered bands coordinate seasonal grazing moves by melody alone. They mourn slaughtered goats with all-night dirges. Lonely travelers follow faint music toward safe paths. They gather river reeds for new pipes yearly. In battle they stampede terrified herds with shrill blasts before charging with copper daggers. They fall completely silent when captured. Their cracked pipes pass down as heirlooms.
Combat: 3/1/4/3, always_physical.
Appearance: pipe-carrying piper with reed-pipes and dagger, horns polished, lungs vast. States: ridge-piping stance / stampede-blast dagger rush / pipe-smashed stagger / silent kneeling surrender. Variants: 2M young piper / grey caller; 2F keen piper / older singer.

## 98. ENY_AEGIPAN_CLIMBER — Aegipan Climber
Sheer-face climbers scale dripping cliffs to rescue stranded goats and steal eagles’ eggs. Their daring bands know every crack and ledge intimately. They trade barred feathers to fletchers for salt. They mock lowlanders’ fear of heights openly. In battle they drop silently from above with short spears, vanishing upward before retaliation. They never fight downhill on flat ground. Their hidden cliff caches shelter newborn kids. Sudden storms strand them miserably.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: rope-laden climber with short spear and egg-net, hooves chalked, arms corded. States: cliff-hang vigil / drop-spear vanish / rope-cut fall stagger / ledge-huddled surrender. Variants: 2M young crag-boy / grey spider; 2F hard climber / older keeper.

## 99. ENY_AEGIPAN_CHEESEWARD — Aegipan Cheeseward
Broad cheesewards guard echoing cave stores where heavy wheels age through winters. Their watchful bands weigh every wheel with jealous care. They sample each batch constantly for ripeness. Eager traders pay premium prices in iron. They hate thieves with lasting passion. In battle they roll burning cheese-wheels downhill before wading in with knotted clubs. They defend mildewed stores to the death. Their echoing caves smell richly of ammonia.
Combat: 4/1/2/3, always_physical.
Appearance: broad cave-guard with cheese-wheel shield and club, whey-stained apron, horns blunt. States: cave-mouth guard / wheel-roll club charge / store-breached frenzy stagger / wheel-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young roller / fat master; 2F hard guard / older affineur.

## 100. ENY_AEGIPAN_ELDER — Aegipan Elder (band-level only)
White-bearded elders settle bitter pasture disputes and remember legendary snow years. Their trembling counsel picks summer ranges each spring. Subordinate bands obey their tapping staffs instantly. They bless newborn kids with thyme smoke. In battle they direct interlocking herd-shields with tall staffs, shielding young behind lowing goats. Their sudden fall scatters herds across three valleys. Their tallies are notched horns kept in order.
Combat: 3/1/4/5, always_physical.
Appearance: white-bearded elder with tall staff and horn-tallies, back bent, eyes milky but sharp. States: staff-tap order / herd-shield direction / staff-broken stagger / seated blessing surrender. Variants: 2M old herd-father / blind singer; 2F old herd-mother / tale-keeper.
