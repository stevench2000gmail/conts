# Humanoid Family 07 — Ogres (5, outside-society bands, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 31. ENY_OGRE — Ogre (reuse)
Hulking ogres drift between bands as muscle for meat, too big for any single tribe to feed long. They stand twice a man’s height, slab-limbed and small-headed, shaped by the same hungry hills that raise brutes without importing their gods. Since imperial granaries failed they have lived on tribute extorted from farms too weak to refuse. Travelers spot their camp-fires first and walk wide. They understand bargains only in food. In battle they swing whole fence-posts, scattering lines through sheer mass, and shrug wounds that drop men. They chase what flees.
Combat: reuse ENY_OGRE (8/1/2/2).
Appearance: towering slab-fighter with fence-post club and hide belt, head small atop mountain shoulders, beside a human like a wall. Bare feet cracked. States: swaying advance / post-sweep crush / shrugged stagger / seated with dropped post. Variants: 2M young lumper / scarred breaker; 2F hard sow / older dam.

## 32. ENY_OGRE_CLUBBER — Ogre Clubber
Clubbers are young ogres hired as living battering rams by hobgoblin files. They love the crash. Quarry towns dread their arrival. They learn timing from sergeants’ shouts. In battle they charge gates and lines head-down, devastating on impact and helpless once turned. Their clubs are whole trees. Hunger makes them reckless. A held gate breaks them.
Combat: 7/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: young tower with whole-tree club and rope harness, eyes eager, shoulders raw. States: head-down paw / tree-swing crash / turned-flail stagger / sprawled on club. Variants: 2M eager youth / scarred ram; 2F hard lass / older matron.

## 33. ENY_OGRE_COOK — Ogre Cook
Cooks are rare ogres who learned fire-tending in army trains, boiling whole cauldrons for bands. They taste everything. Camps fight to keep them. Their stews stretch thin meat. In battle they defend cauldrons with ladles and cleavers the size of swords, furious when pots spill. They feed allies mid-fight with distracted generosity. Their fires mark camps for miles. Rain ruins their craft.
Combat: 6/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: cauldron-tending giant with cleaver-ladle and tasting bowls, apron of sewn hides, nose running. Belly vast. States: pot-stir guard / ladle-crush / scalded stagger / seated over spilled pot. Variants: 2M young taster / fat master; 2F hard cook / older dam.

## 34. ENY_OGRE_THROWER — Ogre Thrower
Throwers are ogres with pitcher’s arms who hurl stones the weight of lambs. They learned skipping rocks across lakes. Outposts fear their range. They carry stone-piles. In battle they hail boulders from ridges then lumber down with fists, avoiding tight work. Their aim fades past fifty paces. Their piles betray positions. Broken fingers end them.
Combat: 7/1/2/2, always_physical.
Appearance: long-armed thrower with stone-pile and sling-belt, one shoulder overbuilt, grin gap-toothed. States: ridge-windup / boulder-hail / finger-crack stagger / seated with empty pile. Variants: 2M young pitcher / scarred hurler; 2F hard thrower / older keeper.

## 35. ENY_OGRE_MATRIARCH — Ogre Matriarch (band-level only)
Matriarchs are eldest ogres holding mixed bands by food-wisdom and sheer bulk. They remember droughts and lead to water. Young brutes obey their ladles. They claim no rule beyond the next meal, kept below faction scale. In battle they wade in with iron-shod staves, shielding young behind bulk. Their fall scatters every hanger-on. Their cauldrons never empty while they live. Rivals wait for winter.
Combat: 7/1/3/4, always_physical.
Appearance: vast elder with iron-shod staff and cauldron-lid shield, grey hair in ropes, hands like hams. Mountainous calm. States: wading advance / staff-sweep shield / knee-buckle stagger / seated giving shares. Variants: 2M kin-guards — sons/brothers (reason: matriarch female-led line, males as guards); 2F old matriarch / daughter-heir.
