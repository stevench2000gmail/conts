# Humanoid Family 09 — Elves (5, flagged exile bands only, no settlements/culture as final, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free, every sentence 8+ words. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 41. ENY_ELF_THORN — Thorn Elf
Grey-clad exile bands haunt deep forest edges beyond the last settled fields. They move silently through undergrowth learned across centuries longer than human memory. After the empire’s foresters vanished, these bands kept old boundary paths without claiming any kingdom. Villagers trade venison for arrowheads while fearing their silent judgment. They share campfires sparingly and vanish before debts can form. In battle they loose long arrows from layered cover, retreating before shields close. They duel only when cornered, with slender knives and bitter precision. Hunger and iron shortage drive them toward farms they despise.
Combat: 4/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: tall slender archer wrapped in grey-green forest cloaks, longbow taller than a human child, eyes pale and watchful. Silver hair bound with thorn-cord, boots soft-soled. States: layered-cover aim stance / whistling volley release / flanked knife-draw stagger / kneeling with empty quiver. Variants: 2M young watcher / scarred ranger; 2F keen sister / older keeper.

## 42. ENY_ELF_DARK — Dark Elf
Cave-mouth exiles dwell in quarry shadows where sunlight thins and old workings breathe cold. Their skin holds ash-grey pallor from generations spent beneath stone without claiming any under-kingdom. They remember imperial mines they once worked for wages now forgotten. Miners trade mushroom-bread for their warning clicks about bad air. They hate bright noise and open battlefields with equal passion. In combat they strike from darkness with short spears, blinking helplessly under noon sun. They ransom captured lanterns for food rather than murder. Their clicking language warns kin across echoing shafts.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: ash-grey slender fighter with short spear and hooded dark cloak, pale eyes wide against lamplight, smaller shadow beside human bulk. Stone-dust ingrained, fingers long. States: darkness-crouch vigil / spear-thrust eruption / sun-blind shielding stagger / groping seated surrender. Variants: 2M young creeper / blind elder; 2F hard mother / older listener.

## 43. ENY_ELF_WOODWARD — Woodward Elf
Antler-crowned wardens patrol ancient groves they tend without owning, marking boar runs. Their bands prune blighted limbs and burn diseased undergrowth each spring. Forest villagers leave salt at boundary stones for safe passage. They remember boundary oaths sworn to bailiffs long dead. They tolerate charcoal-burners while hating clear-cutters with cold fury. In battle they fight with long knives and snares among roots, vanishing between trunks. They never hold ground, preferring to bleed pursuers slowly. Their antler helms are shed finds, never forged crowns.
Combat: 4/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: tall warden wearing shed-antler helm over green-brown bark-cloth, long knife at hip, snare-wire coils across chest. Limbs scarred by briars. States: root-stance watch / snare-spring knife rush / entangled stagger / bound kneeling among roots. Variants: 2M young pruner / scarred warden; 2F keen tender / older grove-keeper.

## 44. ENY_ELF_NIGHTBLADE — Nightblade Elf
Moonlit duelists hire out as bodyguards to caravans crossing haunted forest roads. Their slender blades descend from dueling traditions older than successor courts. Merchants pay double for their night watches. They practice forms alone at dawn while camps still sleep. They despise noisy brawls and challenge hecklers to single combat. In battle they parry and riposte with exact footwork, frail against massed rushes. They withdraw when contracts end, never from fear. Their honor forbids looting the fallen.
Combat: 5/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: moon-pale duelist with slender sword and parrying dagger, midnight-blue cloak clasped with leaf-silver, posture perfect beside human slouch. Hair braided tight. States: formal salute stance / riposte lunge flourish / overrun stagger / kneeling with broken blade. Variants: 2M young pupil / grey master; 2F sharp sister / older mistress.

## 45. ENY_ELF_WANDERER — Wanderer Elf
Road-worn wanderers walk alone between distant bands carrying messages and seeds. Their lifespan lets them remember roads the empire paved and successor tolls abandoned. They trade stories for shelter, never staying past three nights. Villagers call them luck when harvests follow. They avoid entanglements with courts or captains entirely. In battle they fight defensively with staff and short bow, escaping at first openings. They know every ford and famine path. Their seed-pouches replant burned groves quietly.
Combat: 3/1/5/3, always_physical.
Appearance: travel-stained walker with staff and short bow, seed-pouches heavy at belt, cloak patched across centuries. Eyes distant, boots resoled endlessly. States: road-stance readiness / staff-trip bow-shot retreat / pack-drop stagger / seated with raised staff surrender. Variants: 2M young farer / road-grey elder; 2F wandering sister / seed-keeping mother.
