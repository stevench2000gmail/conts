## Order 1 Seraphim — burning light/praise (all Months proposed)
#1 Lucifer (J/C, Jan). RW: morning star, light-bearer. BEFORE: first singer of dawn-praise, bearing holy light to choirs. AFTER: light that blinds and burns; praise curdled into self-worship.
#2 Apollo (Greek, Jun). RW: sun, music, archery, plague. BEFORE: leader of holy song; arrows of righteous judgment. AFTER: plague-arrows for spite; music that maddens; flayer of rivals.
#3 Ra (Egyptian, Jul). RW: sun-creator, daily rebirth. BEFORE: keeper of the dawn gate, lighting the world-temple. AFTER: noon that never ends — scorching stasis; demands worship as the only light.
#4 Jade Emperor (Chinese, Aug; elevated per user). RW: Celestial court sovereign, cosmic bureaucracy. BEFORE: highest regulator of holy liturgy, seating every choir in rank. AFTER: bureaucrat of damnation — souls misfiled, prayers taxed; praise become protocol.

## Order 2 Cherubim — wisdom (all Months proposed)
#5 Thoth (Egyptian, Sep). RW: scribe of gods, magic, weighing of words. BEFORE: recorder of holy law, librarian of true names. AFTER: falsifier — rewrites names in registers; forger of verdicts.
#6 Odin (Norse, Oct). RW: sacrificed eye for wisdom, runes. BEFORE: seeker who paid honestly for holy mysteries. AFTER: wisdom hoarded and weaponized; runes that bind their readers; one-eyed spy.
#7 Athena (Greek, Nov). RW: strategic wisdom, craft. BEFORE: tactician of holy hosts, weaver of victory. AFTER: cold schemer; crafts snares for heroes; wisdom without mercy.
#8 Saraswati (Indian, Dec). RW: music, learning, rivers. BEFORE: tuner of choir voices, keeper of the holy river of song. AFTER: songs that unmake memory; river dammed — knowledge stagnated into dogma.

## Order 3 Thrones — judgment (2 Months + 2 Hours proposed)
#9 Anubis (Egyptian, Feb). RW: embalming, feather-scale judgment. BEFORE: tender of holy dead, weigher of true hearts. AFTER: weigher who rigs the scales; embalmer of the living. Triple-note: folk (Humanoid jackal-headed) vs animal (Beast jackals) vs fiend (Fiend Jackal Husk).
#10 Azazel (J/C, Mar). RW: scapegoat bore sins to wilderness, bound till judgment. BEFORE: bearer-away of confessed sins (holy sanitation). AFTER: sins returned to senders with interest; wilderness swallowing pilgrims.
#11 Shamash (Mesopotamian, Hour IX). RW: solar justice, law. BEFORE: lamp of righteous courts, exposing hidden wrongs. AFTER: pitiless exposure — no mercy, no dusk; justice as vengeance.
#12 Tyr (Norse, Hour XVIII). RW: bound Fenrir at cost of hand, oath-keeper. BEFORE: guarantor of holy oaths, hostage of peace. AFTER: oath-twister; peace-hostage turned jailer.

## Order 4 Dominions — rulership (2 Months + 2 Hours proposed)
#13 Zeus (Greek, May). RW: king of gods, thunder, guest-law. BEFORE: high governor seating lower orders, keeper of guest-law. AFTER: tyrant of shifting moods; hospitality turned ambush; thunderbolts for vanity. (Zepar-flagged distinct.)
#14 Amaterasu (Japanese, Hour VI; lowered per user). RW: sun queen, cave-hiding. BEFORE: radiant governor whose presence ordered the courts. AFTER: withdrew light into the cave — sullen eclipse; blessing only flatterers.
#15 Marduk (Babylonian, Hour XII). RW: slew Tiamat, ordered cosmos. BEFORE: champion-governor chaining the abyss. AFTER: order as iron cage; abyss-things re-hired as enforcers.
#16 Sojobo Great Tengu (Japanese, April LOCKED; mountain rule). RW: king of tengu, mountains, swordsmanship. BEFORE: warden-governor of high passes, testing pilgrims fairly. AFTER: tests become torments; mountain rule absolute.

## Order 5 Virtues — nature/miracles (all Hours proposed)
#17 Thor (Norse, Hour III). RW: thunder, protector of farmers/Midgard. BEFORE: holy weather-warden, hallower of fields. AFTER: storms for sport; protector turned protection-racketeer.
#18 Sun Wukong (Chinese, Hour XV; IN per user, replaces Susanoo). RW: trickster-sage, 72 transformations, staff, scripture pilgrimage. BEFORE: miracle-worker of impossible rescues; staff bridging abysses. AFTER: transformations to torment; staff shattering gates; pilgrimage sabotaged from inside.
#19 Indra (Indian, Hour XI). RW: thunder, rain, soma. BEFORE: rain-bringer general of holy hosts. AFTER: drought-maker and flood-maker by whim; soma become stupor.
#20 Set (Egyptian, Hour XXII). RW: desert storms, strength, fratricide. BEFORE: holy violence at borders — nightly slayer of serpent Apophis. AFTER: serpent-slayer befriended the serpent; border violence turned inward.

## Order 6 Powers — warfare/protection (all Hours proposed)
#21 Samael (J/C, Hour XIX). RW: angel of death, accuser, venom. BEFORE: holy executioner — death dealt justly and cleanly. AFTER: death dealt joyfully and dirtily; venom in every cup.
#22 Ares (Greek, Hour II). RW: battle-frenzy, courage. BEFORE: holy wrath against true enemies; shield of the outnumbered. AFTER: war for its own sake; both sides blessed, both bled.
