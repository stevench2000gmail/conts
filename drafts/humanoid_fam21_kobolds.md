# Humanoid Family 21 — Kobolds (5, high-count, small scavengers, separate from lizard stalkers), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 86. ENY_KOBOLD_SCRAPPER — Kobold Scrapper
Small scavenger bands infest stinking refuse pits clustered outside busy walled settlements. Their yapping packs drag fallen offal away promptly at dusk. They worship shiny scrap metal with hoarding reverence. They fear large housecats far more than human guards. In battle they swarm victims with sharpened spoons and awls, overwhelming through sheer numbers. They scatter squealing immediately at presented torches. Their reeking pits betray every nearby lair. Starved winter months drive them desperately indoors.
Combat: 2/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: rat-sized dog-like scavenger with spoon-shiv and scrap satchel, standing knee-high to humans, tail wagging nervously. Mangy fur. States: pit-lurk crouch / yapping swarm stab / torch-panic scatter stagger / belly-up whimpering surrender. Variants: 2M young yapper / scarred snatcher; 2F quick dam / older hoarder.

## 87. ENY_KOBOLD_TRAPPER — Kobold Trapper
Clever wire-layers defend narrow warren mouths with strung bells and vicious foot snares. Their timid bands trade early warnings to stronger neighbors for food scraps. They learn trap-craft from watching human hunters. They hate burrowing badgers with lasting passion. In battle they ring clanging alarms before stoning invaders from low tunnels, never facing anyone openly. Their tangling wires catch friends as often as foes. Sudden floods drown entire warrens helplessly.
Combat: 2/1/5/2, always_physical.
Appearance: wire-laden small setter with bell-strings and stone-pouch, ears huge, eyes bright. States: mouth-crouch listen / alarm-ring stone-hail / wire-tangled stagger / bell-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young ringer / grey layer; 2F quick setter / older listener.

## 88. ENY_KOBOLD_HOUNDMASTER — Kobold Houndmaster
Mangy houndmasters lead ragged packs of half-wild curs through refuse-choked alleys. Their scrawny bands hunt teeming rats for every meal. They share all meat evenly with their dogs. In battle they loose snarling dogs first before jabbing with short spears. Fallen dogs rout handlers instantly. Their makeshift kennels move with each eviction. Fresh meat alone sustains doubtful loyalty.
Combat: 3/1/3/2 (handler+dogs), always_physical.
Appearance: cur-master with leash-tangles and short spear, curs snapping around knees, standing small among dogs. States: pack-release stance / spear-jab behind dogs / dog-loss wail stagger / leash-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young caller / scarred master; 2F hard caller / older breeder.

## 89. ENY_KOBOLD_TINKER — Kobold Tinker
Scrap-tinkers mend discarded broken tools with twisted wire and muttered prayers. Their hopeful bands haunt busy smithy yards after closing hours. They love salvaged hinges above silver coins. In battle they swing light hammers weakly while attempting repairs mid-fight absurdly. Their rattling carts hold unsorted junk piles. Cold rain rusts their entire stock quickly.
Combat: 2/1/4/2, always_physical. (Note: 6-sentence sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: hinge-laden mender with hammer and wire-spools, apron trailing ground, eyes hopeful. States: cart-lean offer / hammer-tap swing / cart-topple stagger / junk-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young fixer / grey saver; 2F quick mender / older keeper.

## 90. ENY_KOBOLD_CHIEF — Kobold Chief (band-level only)
Yapping chiefs hold crowded warrens through sheer loudness of voice. Their constant boasts rally timid packs to risky raids. They claim polished old boots as crowns. In battle they screech orders while waving bent swords from atop trash thrones, dueling rival yappers personally. Their sudden fall scatters packs into hiding instantly. Rival chiefs absorb leaderless survivors quickly.
Combat: 3/1/3/4, always_physical. (Note: 7-sentence sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: boot-crowned small chief with bent sword and trash throne, voice shrill, chest puffed. States: throne-stand screech / sword-wave duel / throne-topple stagger / boot-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young yapper / scarred holder; 2F hard dam-chief / older matron.
