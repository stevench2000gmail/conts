# Humanoid Family 24 — Boar-headed brutes (5, high-count; WHF-beastman texture only, no lore import; distinct from Beast boars animals), Phase 1, no JSON
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 101. ENY_BOARHEAD_TUSKER — Boar Tusker
Bristle-maned tusker bands root through forest loam beyond the last orchards. Their heavy heads swing low, scenting truffles and travelers alike. They learned shield-breaking charging through boar-infested thickets. Farmers dread their autumn hunger. They collect fallen helmets as bowls. They fear fire lines. In battle they charge head-down with cleavers, goring with tusks. They trample fallen foes.
Combat: 6/1/2/2, always_physical. (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: bristle-maned boar-headed fighter with cleaver and helmet-bowl, tusks curved, bulk broad, snout steaming. States: loam-sniffing advance / head-down gore charge / fire-turned squeal stagger / wallow-seated surrender. Variants: 2M young grunter / scarred boar; 2F hard sow / older keeper.

## 102. ENY_BOARHEAD_ARCHER — Boar Archer
Short-limbed archers loose from forest edge blinds with surprising patience. Their bands cover tusker charges. They fletch with shed bristles. They guard piglets fiercely. In battle they volley then squeal-retreat behind trees, avoiding melee. Their strings snap in wet. Their blinds smell strongly.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical. (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: blind-hidden archer with short bow and bristle-fletched shafts, snout quivering, ears pricked. States: blind-wait aim / volley squeal-retreat / blind-burned stagger / snout-down surrender. Variants: 2M young bow / grey eye; 2F keen sow / older fletcher.

## 103. ENY_BOARHEAD_SPEAKER — Boar Speaker (no true magic)
Mud-painted speakers grunt prophecies over truffle finds. Their authority rests on scent-reading and loud squeals, never sorcery. Bands follow their snouts to food. They anoint champions with loam. In battle they squeal charges then jab with staves, rallying young. Their fall quiets herds. Their mud-masks crack in sun.
Combat: 3/1/4/4, cycle (squeal-then-jab, mundane). (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: mud-masked grunter with prophecy staff and truffle-pouch, paint cracked, breath hot. States: sniffing prophecy stance / squeal-charge staff jab / mask-washed stagger / mud-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young sniffer / grey grunt; 2F hard sow-speaker / older dam.

## 104. ENY_BOARHEAD_RAIDER — Boar Raider
Armored raiders ride huge feral hogs, crashing through hedges. Their cavalry terrifies villages. They shoe their mounts with iron. Farriers dread them. In battle they ride down lines with lances, wheeling for second passes. Dismounted they flounder. Their hogs eat anything.
Combat: 5/1/3/3 (rider+mount). (Note: condensed sketch in chat; locked content identical.)
Appearance: lance-armed rider atop a feral war-hog, armor patched, tusks filed, both snorting. States: hedge-pawing trot / crashing lance pass / unseated wallow stagger / mount-offering surrender. Variants: 2M young rider / scarred chief; 2F hard rider / older breeder.

## 105. ENY_BOARHEAD_CHIEF — Boar Chief (band-level only)
Enormous chiefs hold grunting sounders together through sheer weight and insatiable appetite. Their nightly feasts decide the next dawn’s raids. Young challengers test their tusks constantly. In battle they duel enemy champions personally with great cleavers while sounders clash around. Their sudden death splits sounders into feuding splinters. Their steaming middens grow larger each year. A consistently full trough keeps fragile peace.
Combat: 7/1/2/4, always_physical.
Appearance: enormous tusked chief with great cleaver and trough-throne, bulk dwarfing kin, scars layered. States: trough-stand challenge / great-cleaver duel / tusk-cracked stagger / trough-yielding surrender. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred holder; 2F hard matron / older sow-queen.
