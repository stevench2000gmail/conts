# Humanoid Family 05 — Hobgoblins (5, outside-society bands, Phase 1, no JSON)
Rule: city-free. Taxonomy Humanoid.

## 21. ENY_HOBGOBLIN_BRUTE — Hobgoblin Brute
Brutes are tall goblin-kin who sell disciplined muscle to any band that feeds. They stand near human height, orange-skinned and tusked, keeping crude drill learned aping old imperial manuals. Villagers fear their patrol lines more than goblin packs. They keep ranks where lesser kin mob. In battle they advance with halberds in lockstep, holding where goblins flee and withdrawing only on horn-calls. Their pay is meat and first sleep. Empty camps scatter them.
Combat: 5/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: tall orange fighter with halberd and rank-cloak, tusked jaw, scarred knuckles, nearly human-statured. Broad shoulders. States: rank-advance / halberd-sweep / rank-break stagger / kneeling with grounded pole. Variants: 2M young levy / scarred fileman; 2F hard fighter / older matron.

## 22. ENY_HOBGOBLIN_ARCHER — Hobgoblin Archer
Archer files loose disciplined volleys from scrub ridges, fletching their own shafts. Their craft recalls grim archer lines without claiming any chaos taint. Caravans dread their ranging whistles. They range every trail. In battle they shoot from cover then fall back with short swords, avoiding melee. Their strings are oiled nightly. Rain slows them badly. Their notched shafts mark kills.
Combat: 4/1/4/2, always_physical.
Appearance: ridge archer with longbow and shaft-bundle, eye-scar, ridge-cloak, tall frame bent to aim. States: ridge-aim / volley-loose / sword-draw stagger / kneeling with empty bundle. Variants: 2M young bow / grey eye; 2F keen lass / older keeper.

## 23. ENY_HOBGOBLIN_SERGEANT — Hobgoblin Sergeant (band-level)
Sergeants drill mixed goblin packs with shouts and cudgels, turning mobs into files. They count heads and meat-shares on notched sticks. Bands march straighter and steal more under them. They rose from brutes by surviving. In battle they stand shouting orders while cracking cudgel-swords, anchoring lines that would otherwise melt. Their fall routs packs instantly. New sergeants rise by the same cudgel.
Combat: 4/1/4/4, always_physical.
Appearance: thick-throated driver with cudgel-sword and tally-sticks, scarred voice-box, bulk looming over goblins. States: stick-raised order / cudgel-crack / throat-hit stagger / seated giving orders. Variants: 2M young driver / grey tyrant; 2F hard driver / older dam.

## 24. ENY_HOBGOBLIN_RAIDER — Hobgoblin Raider
Raiders ride scavenged ponies to outflank patrols and burn stores. They learn ground the way outriders do, circling wide. Outposts fear dawn dust. They scout by night and strike at first light. In battle they circle slashing with sabers, never standing to trade blows. Their ponies tire fast and limit range. Fodder dictates campaigns. A lame horse grounds the whole file.
Combat: 4/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: mounted tall raider with saber and dust-cloak, stolen saddle, pony shaggy. High in stirrups. States: circling trot / saber-slash / unhorsed sprawl / grounded holding reins. Variants: 2M young rider / bearded chief; 2F hard rider / older wrangler.

## 25. ENY_HOBGOBLIN_WARLORD — Hobgoblin Warlord (band-level only, no faction)
Warlords hold large bands by victories and meat-feasts, challenged nightly by young brutes. They wear trophy-cloaks and keep iron helms. Bands follow winners without question. They claim no territory or tribute-law, only the next raid, kept deliberately below faction scale. In battle they seek enemy champions with great swords, dueling while files clash around. Their death splits bands into rival packs that stronger neighbors absorb. A full belly keeps uneasy peace longer than oaths.
Combat: 6/1/3/4, always_physical.
Appearance: great-sword chief with trophy-cloak and iron helm, scarred bulk a head over kin, teeth filed. Imposing. States: challenge-stance / great-cut / helm-rattle stagger / kneeling with grounded sword. Variants: 2M young challenger / scarred holder; 2F hard chief / older matriarch.
