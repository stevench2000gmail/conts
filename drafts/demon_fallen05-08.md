## 5. ENY_ODIN — Odin the One-Eyed
The fifth Fallen once acquired and preserved knowledge for the proper guidance of Creation. After wisdom lost restraint, no sacrifice or boundary is unacceptable in his pursuit of knowing. Ordinary oath-makers spit over left shoulders fearfully when ravens gather in pairs. In battle he names every secret aloud from memory, unmanning lines before striking with spears. No gallows teaches him anything twice ever, for hanged truths resurrect as his servants. Runesters debate whether his missing eye sees more now than two ever did.

## 6. ENY_THOTH — Thoth the Measurer

No library admits his ibis shadow willingly anymore, for catalogued books rearrange themselves overnight. Baboon chatter in temple courts warns keepers hourly of approaching tallies.

## 7. ENY_SARASWATI — Saraswati of the Veena

## 8. ENY_ATHENA — Athena Grey-Eyes

Ordinary city councils vote with olive branches raised fearfully when her owls perch on roofs. In battle she solves ranks like diagrams coldly, removing pieces before striking with spears. No plan survives her audit intact ever, for each correction demands costlier obedience. Veterans debate whether her mercy or her arithmetic failed first on forgotten fields.
