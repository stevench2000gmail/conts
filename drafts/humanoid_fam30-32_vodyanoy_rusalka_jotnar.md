## 125. ENY_VODYANOY_OLD — Old Vodyanoy
Bloated green-bearded elders squat in millpond depths beneath rotting waterwheels. Their patient bands drown millers who cheat river customs deliberately. They remember every dam built across three generations. Fisherfolk offer tobacco at muddy banks cautiously. They collect drowned brass clocks obsessively. They hate drained channels fiercely. In battle they drag victims downward with weed-ropes, holding breath incredibly long. They release those who drain cups respectfully.
Combat: 5/1/3/3, always_physical.
Appearance: Bloated green-bearded elder carries weed-ropes and a brass-clock collection, belly vast, webbed fingers dripping steadily. Eyes like pond stones. States: millpond-depth squatting vigil / weed-rope dragging assault / drained-channel gasping stagger / tobacco-accepting releasing surrender. Variants: 4M young mudder / scarred drowner / grey pond-father / old wheel-keeper (male water-lines, females absent with reason: drowned patriarchs).

## 126. ENY_VODYANOY_MILLER — Miller Vodyanoy

In battle they batter intruders with long millstaves from slippery mossy beams, pushing foes into millraces. They flee immediately from deliberately drained leats. Their grey flour always tastes strongly of mud.

Appearance: Grease-slick miller carries a long millstaff and toll-sack, beard tangled with weed, shoulders broad. Mill-dust coats everything. States: beam-perched slippery vigil / millstaff-battering pushing assault / leat-drained flopping stagger / toll-accepting grunting surrender. Variants: 4M young grinder / scarred wheelman / grey sluicer / old mill-father.
