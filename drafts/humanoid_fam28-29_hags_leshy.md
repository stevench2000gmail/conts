## 121. ENY_HAG_OUTCAST — Hag Outcast
Wart-skinned outcast hags dwell alone in leaking hovels beyond the last village wells. Their crooked fingers gather bitter herbs that honest wives refuse to touch. They remember slights across many long decades without forgiving. Villagers blame them loudly for every stillbirth and failed crop. They trade dubious charms for bread crusts. They keep one-eyed cats as sole companions. In battle they curse loudly while striking with iron ladles, fighting with desperate strength. They surrender quickly when grandchildren intervene tearfully.

## 122. ENY_HAG_MIRE — Mire Hag
Bog-dwelling mire hags nest upon floating peat islands inside trackless fens. Their bloated fingers weave eel-traps from human hair scavenged downstream. They lure travelers nightly with false lantern lights over water. Drained marsh crews fear the sucking pools surrounding each island. They collect drowned boots as trophies beside their dripping beds. They understand tides better than most experienced river pilots. Their hooked poles drag victims into black water during battle. They release prisoners who correctly solve their patient riddles.
Combat: 4/3/5/2, cycle (mire curses flagged proposal per magic doctrine; never final).
Appearance: Bloated peat-stained elder carries a hooked pole and lantern-cage, hair dripping, breath marsh-gas foul. Skin green-grey. States: peat-island floating vigil / pole-drag drowning assault / lantern-smashed shrieking stagger / riddle-answered releasing surrender. Variants: 4F young bog-wife / prime lure / grey weaver / old peat-mother.

## 123. ENY_LESHY_WARDEN — Leshy Warden (fixed)
Moss-bearded forest wardens patrol ancient timber stands they solemnly claim to protect. Their towering frames shift subtly among dark trunks, confusing even seasoned woodsmen completely. They punish greedy poachers by leading them in hopeless circles for days. Charcoal-burners leave sweet honey offerings respectfully at mossy boundary stumps. They marry no one and accumulate no property anywhere. They understand roaming wolf packs intimately across many seasons. In battle they ambush intruders with heavy root-clubs from living cover, vanishing silently between trees instantly. They release captives freely who willingly replant young saplings afterward.

Appearance: Moss-bearded towering guardian carries a heavy root-club and honey-gourd, bark-like skin creaking audibly, eyes green-gold and watchful. Height seems variable within shifting forest light always.
States: trunk-merged silent vigil / root-club ambushing assault / fire-menaced roaring stagger / sapling-planting releasing surrender. Variants: 2M old warden / grey misleader; 2F hard wife / older keeper.

## 124. ENY_LESHY_HERDER — Leshy Herder (fixed)
Mushroom-crowned herders drive clicking beetle herds and frog swarms through damp autumn woods. Their shrill piping calls gather every creeping thing for many miles around. They trade glowing shelf fungi to travelers for precious iron nails. Unwary travelers follow their drifting lights helplessly astray at night. They sleep curled inside spacious hollow oaks during daylight hours. They count tree rings meticulously every single spring. In battle they overwhelm foes with swarming bites directed by pipe-tunes, avoiding direct blows personally always. They scatter frantically whenever their fragile pipes break suddenly.
Combat: 3/1/5/3, cycle (swarm-pipe behavior-magic flagged intuitive proposal per magic doctrine; never cast).
Appearance: Mushroom-crowned slight herder carries carved bone pipes and bulging spore-pouches, skin constantly damp, voice high and trilling musically. Strong fungal scent clings heavily to everything nearby.
States: hollow-oak piping stance / swarm-directing overwhelming assault / pipe-snapped panicked stagger / spore-scattering pleading surrender. Variants: 2M young piper / grey caller; 2F piping sister / older brood-keeper.
