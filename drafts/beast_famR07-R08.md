## 43. ENY_PIT_VIPER — Grey Pit-Viper
Stocky grey vipers coil beneath timber stacks where mice nest thickest before winter frosts. They sense warmth through facial pits unerringly, striking gloved hands that lift boards incautiously. After timber tolls slowed lumber movement downhill, their undisturbed broods multiplied inside seasoning stacks. Stackers rap boards with mallets hourly, warning coiled mothers before lifting planks for carts. Ordinary apprentices dread unstacking duty most, for bites swell arms painfully for many days. In battle they hold ground stubbornly, delivering repeated puncturing bites at ankle height rapidly. Thick leggings and hooked poles keep handlers safe effectively during sorting work every morning. Hogs and hard freezes thin numbers yearly, though deep stacks shelter breeding coils through winters.
Combat: 4/1/4/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Thick grey viper coils forearm-long, triangular head broad distinctly beyond a narrow neck. Heat pits dot faces visibly, scales keeled roughly, tail vibrates dryly before every strike. States: stack-coiled heat-sensing ambush / holding ankle-biting repeat assault / pole-lifted dangling stagger / hog-trampled crushed defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, pit-forms only, reason: viper sexes differ only by tail taper): grey juvenile coiler / timber-stack elder / blunt-tail male-presenting / tapering-tail female-presenting.

## 44. ENY_CINDER_VIPER — Cinder Pit-Viper
Dark cinder vipers bask on volcanic ledges where morning sun strikes basalt earliest each dawn. They vanish into cracks instantly when shadows fall, reemerging only after warmth returns fully. After obsidian gatherers fenced richer flows uphill, their basking terraces became contested gathering ground. Gatherers stamp heavily before kneeling deliberately, vibrating warnings that clear coils from work sites. Ordinary porters carry antivenom charcoal religiously now, having watched comrades fall to swift bites. In battle they sidewind across ash rapidly, striking wrists that swing picks without watching ground. High boots and watchful partners prevent most envenomations effectively during quarry work every shift. Shrikes and sudden rockfalls thin numbers locally, though deep fissures preserve breeders through eruptions.
Combat: 5/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Soot-black viper blends with basalt perfectly, heat pits glowing faintly amber in shadow. Body short and powerfully muscled, strike blurringly fast, scales matte against reflective stone. States: ledge-basking coiled vigil / sidewinding wrist-striking rush assault / stamp-warned retreating stagger / rockfall-crushed limp defeat. Variants (sexes subtle, cinder-forms only, reason: cinder viper sexes differ only by girth): ash-grey juvenile basker / basalt-black elder / thick-coiled male-presenting / slender brooding female-presenting.

## 45. ENY_HYDRA_PUP — Two-Headed Hydra Pup
Unfortunate two-headed pups hatch rarely inside marsh nests where inbreeding pools stagnate across generations. Twin heads snap at each other constantly, complicating hunts that single-headed siblings manage easily. After bounty hunters prized oddities for menageries downhill, their frightened mothers hid nests deeper inside reeds. Reed-cutters report nest sites for coppers quietly, allowing collectors to cage pups without killing mothers. Ordinary villagers view pups as dire omens, leaving salt offerings to avert imagined coming floods. In battle they bite erratically from both heads, tearing leggings with uncoordinated but painful nips. A forked stick pinning one neck calms attacks effectively, allowing handlers to bag specimens safely. Hunger and sibling rivalry kill most pups quickly, though collectors preserve survivors in brine pits.
Combat: 3/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Small marsh snake writhes with twin heads divergently, each crowned separately and quarrelsome. Bodies green-brown and stunted, scales mismatched, movement jerky from competing impulses constantly. States: nest-hidden quarrelsome waiting / erratic twin-biting nip assault / stick-pinned subdued stagger / brine-pitted preserved defeat. Variants (defect-forms only, reason: hydra pups are sterile defects without sexes): left-dominant snapper / right-dominant snapper / equal-headed writher / stunted runt twin.

## 46. ENY_HYDRA_LITTER — Three-Headed Litter Runt
Rarer three-headed runts survive only through human charity inside menagerie pits near market towns. Keepers feed minnows by hand daily patiently, separating heads with wooden dividers during every meal. After crowds paid coppers to witness feedings eagerly, their upkeep became profitable for clever owners. Owners parade runts on feast days carefully, muzzling two heads while allowing one to sway. Ordinary children adore triple swaying while priests warn against worshipping accidents of stagnant blood. In battle they thrash wildly in all directions, lashing tails and necks with surprising strength. Thick sacks thrown over coils entangle heads effectively, ending resistance without harming valuable specimens. Disease and starvation claim captives often, though careful warming preserves favorites through cold winters.
Combat: 4/1/1/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Stunted triple-headed oddity coils basket-sized, central head largest with flanking dwarfs attached. Scales dull and patchy, eyes cloudy differing, tongues flicker asynchronously without coordination. States: pit-coiled displayed curiosity / thrashing triple-lashing entanglement assault / sack-smothered subdued stagger / disease-wasted still defeat. Variants (defect-forms only, reason: triple runts are sterile defects without sexes): central-dominant thrasher / left-flanker biter / right-flanker hisser / patch-scaled runt survivor.
