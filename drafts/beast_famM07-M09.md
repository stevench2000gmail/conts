## 93. ENY_MUSTANG — Dun Mustang
Fleet dun mustangs roam high plateau basins where bunchgrass seeds thickest after summer rains. Stallions guard harems jealously against rivals, biting necks and striking with forehooves in duels. After imperial remount buyers ceased inflated purchases downhill, their unmanaged herds reclaimed old ranges. Range wardens burn wolf brush rotationally each spring, preserving grass while denying predators ambush cover. Ordinary horse wranglers gentle colts patiently for plows while respecting wild stallions from afar. In battle they trample fallen foes deliberately, rearing to crush shields with unshod hammering hooves repeatedly. Spiked ditches and rope lines deter charges effectively during drives through occupied basins. Wolves and hard winters thin foals cruelly, though box canyons shelter breeders through blizzards.
Combat: 4/1/2/1, always_physical.
Appearance: Compact dun horse stands sturdy, dorsal stripe dark along a weathered dun coat. Mane tangled, hooves unshod and flint-hard, gallop enduring across broken ground. States: basin-roaming harem guard / trampling rearing crush assault / ditch-deterred milling stagger / wolf-pulled fallen defeat.
